# s1 research notes — Colorado license law & Commission (verified 2026-10-12)

Bash egress to dre.colorado.gov blocked (proxy 403); facts gathered via WebFetch summaries. Rule text via LII (law.cornell.edu) copy of 4 CCR 725-1.

## Commission / Division
- C.R.S. 12-10-206 (https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_12-10-206): 5 members appointed by governor; 3 brokers with ≥5 yrs Colorado real estate experience (1 with substantial property mgmt experience); 2 public members; 3-year terms; governor may remove for misconduct, neglect of duty, incompetence; vacancies filled for unexpired term; "type 1" entity.
- 12-10-207: Division of Real Estate; director employs staff, enforces and prosecutes, conducts audits of licensees' business accounts.
- 12-10-217 (https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_12-10-217): investigate on own motion or written complaint. Penalties after hearing: fine up to $2,500 per separate offense, censure, probation (terms set by Commission), temporary suspension, permanent revocation. Letter of admonition by certified mail; licensee may request formal proceedings in writing within 20 days after receipt (letter vacated). Revoked: no new application considered for 1 year. Investigative files/complaints closed; stipulations and final orders public. Firm's right to operate can be suspended/revoked. Grounds list incl. (a) misrepresentation/false advertising, (e) acting for more than one party without knowledge of all, (h)/(i) failing to account, commingling, (j) failing to provide closing statements/duplicates, (k) 4-yr records, (l) paying unlicensed persons, (n) convictions (deferred judgments count), (o) fair housing, (q) unworthiness/incompetency, (r) failing to supervise, (s) license by fraud, (t) secret compensation, (u) option without disclosure of profit, (w) dishonest dealing, (x) discipline elsewhere.
- 12-10-219: ALJ conducts hearings (APA 24-4-104/105); Commission decides by majority vote; review by Colorado Court of Appeals (24-4-106(11)).
- 12-10-222: broker disciplined for employee's acts only if actual knowledge or negligent supervision.
- 12-10-223: unlicensed brokerage, or acting while revoked/suspended = class 2 misdemeanor. (No fine amounts verified — omitted.)
- 12-10-226: Commission may seek court injunction.
- Recovery fund: repealed 2005 — Colorado Session Laws 2005 ch. 177 "Concerning the Repeal of the Real Estate Recovery Fund" (https://scholar.law.colorado.edu/session-laws-2001-2050/2077; bill text https://www.statebillinfo.com/bills/bills/05/1264_rer.pdf). No recovery fund section in current Part 2 (https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_title_12_article_10).
- Position statements: Real Estate Manual ch. 3 intro — CPs are guidance/best practices, "not law" (https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/2026_CREM_Ch%2003.pdf; list at https://dre.colorado.gov/real-estate-manual-and-position-statements).

## Licensing
- 12-10-201 definitions/exemptions (https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_12-10-201): broker = for compensation, sells/exchanges/buys/rents/leases, offers, negotiates, lists, auctions, option dealing, business opportunity w/ real estate, soliciting fee from prospective tenant for rental info. Single transaction suffices. Exemptions: unpaid attorney-in-fact under POA; public officials; receivers/trustees/executors/guardians; attorneys for clients; owners selling own property; corporation through officers/salaried employees; salaried on-site apartment/condo managers; out-of-state broker receiving cooperative share from Colorado broker; utility/right-of-way acquisition, etc.
- 12-10-203: age 18; 168 hrs (48 law & practice, 48 contracts, 72 trust accounts/record keeping, closings, current legal issues, practical applications) or real estate degree; fingerprints to CBI before application; entity designates broker; temporary license up to 90 days + one 90-day extension when designated broker leaves; employed broker can't hold license under trade name; (5)(c): 2 yrs active before independent practice, high level of supervision during that period; 24 hrs brokerage administration before employing others.
- Rules ch. 2 (https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/colorado/title-4/agency-725/division-1/chapter-2): Independent Broker = 2 yrs Active as Associate Broker; Employing Broker = 2 yrs active within prior 5 yrs + 50 experience points + 24-hr Brokerage Administration; exam passing score valid 1 year (12-10-212 too).
- Rules ch. 1 definitions: Associate Broker works under Employing Broker; Independent Broker operates own firm, supervises no associates; Supervisory Broker = delegated in writing; Team = 2+ brokers same firm; Unlicensed On-Site Manager salaried/IC paid through firm, non-commission.
- Rules ch. 3 (https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/colorado/title-4/agency-725/division-1/chapter-3): initial license expires Dec 31 of year issued; then calendar 3-yr cycle; if E&O proof or Employing Broker not provided within 30 days of notice, license issued Inactive; Rule 3.10 Colorado place of business for Independent/Employing Brokers residing in CO (associate & inactive exempt); reinstatement within 31 days = renewal fee; 31 days–1 yr = + half fee; 1–3 yrs = + full fee; >3 yrs = new applicant (also 12-10-212). Former employing broker may pay inactive/transferred broker for commissions earned while active.
- E&O Rule 3.9 + 12-10-204: all active brokers; inactive and attorneys with malpractice coverage exempt; Division group plan by competitive bid; independent policy allowed: ≥$100,000 per claim, ≥$300,000 aggregate, defense costs outside limits/first-dollar, deductible ≤$1,000, lockbox ≥$25,000, 365-day extended reporting; firm policy $1M/$1M, deductible ≤$10,000; lapse = immediate Inactive.
- CE (https://dre.colorado.gov/broker-continuing-education; 12-10-213): 24 hrs per 3-yr cycle; 12 hrs = three different annual 4-hr Annual Commission Update courses (one each year); missed ACU can't be made up (alternatives: 24-hr Broker Reactivation course, pass state exam, or 72 hrs); no CE for initial partial period; inactive >3 yrs → state exam or 72 hrs to reactivate; keep CE proof 4 years.
- 12-10-211: immediate notice of change in business location or employment; failure → automatic inactivation; both parties notify on termination; employing broker holds custody of employed broker's license. 12-10-212: employing broker's license suspended/revoked/expired → employees' licenses automatically inactive.
- Rule 6.23: written notice within 30 calendar days of conviction/plea, fair housing violation, discipline elsewhere.

## Conduct (Rules ch. 6 https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/colorado/title-4/agency-725/division-1/chapter-6)
- 6.1 license not transferable; can't lend license; associate can't hold out as employing.
- 6.2 competency: decline, acquire, get help, co-list.
- 6.3 supervision: all associates + unlicensed staff; reasonable vs high-level (new associates); delegation in writing; employing broker ultimately responsible.
- 6.4 written brokerage relationship policy; Office Policy Manual signed by each associate.
- 6.10 advertising: name in Commission records; firm name clear & conspicuous (exempt: own unlisted property); one trade name filed; team names can't use realty/real estate/company/Inc./LLC etc.; team ads include firm name; each web page shows firm name; expired listings removed within 3 days; social media firm name (first click if limited space); others' listings only with written permission.
- 6.13 offers presented to listing broker (then employing broker; then directly).
- 6.14 duplicates at time of preparation; no separate fee for legal documents; listings in writing before services; definite termination date; holdover clause limited to names submitted in writing during term; don't advise on title exceptions (UPL).
- 6.15 sign crossing. 6.16 access info. 6.17 conflicts in writing; own account license disclosure; property manager vendor interests.
- 6.18 affiliated business disclosure. 6.20 files 4 yrs. 6.21 referral fees/RESPA; out-of-state referral conditions. 6.25 respond to complaints/audits; failure is grounds even if complaint lacks merit. 6.26 7-day consumer notice, 30-day disbursements.
- 12-10-221: employed broker paid only by employing broker.
- 12-10-403(4): standard forms; must use Commission-approved form when one exists; advise forms have legal consequences, consult counsel. Conway-Bogue Realty v. Denver Bar Ass'n (Colo. 1957): filling in standard forms incident to broker's own transaction, no extra charge (https://www.ltgc.com/resources/document-preparation/ — secondary).
- CP-2/CP-4/CP-7/CP-8/CP-14/CP-18/CP-20/CP-22 (Manual ch. 3): rebates permitted, buyer rebate disclosed to lender & on settlement statement, never outside closing; listings belong to firm; brokers buying own listings strongly discouraged; client picks settlement provider; REAP permitted/prohibited tasks; contingent pay to unlicensed REAP may violate RESPA.
- CP-23: lease options/installment contracts — use Colorado attorney; no Commission-approved form. MLO definition: takes residential loan application or offers/negotiates residential loan terms (PS 1.8).
- Net listings: Colorado Real Estate Manual (2007 ed., https://spl.cde.state.co.us/artemis/regserials/reg52908r22internet/2007%20manual/reg52908r22200716internet.pdf) — not expressly illegal, out of favor, no approved form, courts may limit to usual commission, secret profit risk. Not found in current rules. (Older source — reviewer check.)
