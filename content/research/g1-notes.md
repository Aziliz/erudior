# g1 research notes (federal tax, construction loans, appraiser regulation, ARELLO)

Checked 2026-10-12. Notes paraphrased.

## Home-sale exclusion (IRC 121)
- Up to $250,000 gain excluded; $500,000 on a joint return. Ownership test: owned 24 months of the 5 years ending on sale date. Use test: lived in it 24 months in that window (for $500k, each spouse meets use). The two 24-month periods need not coincide. Generally can't use if you excluded gain on another home within prior 2 years; reduced exclusion possible for job change, health, unforeseen circumstances. No lifetime limit on number of uses.
  - https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc701
  - https://www.irs.gov/node/19086 (IRS FAQ, basis/sale of home)
- Gain = amount realized (price less selling expenses) − adjusted basis. Adjusted basis = cost + capital improvements − casualty losses/other decreases, and minus depreciation (allowed or allowable) for any rental/business use. Paying off the mortgage doesn't change gain.
- Gain equal to depreciation after May 6, 1997 can't be excluded; may be taxed at up to 25% (unrecaptured §1250 gain). https://www.irs.gov/node/17558

## Capital gains basics
- Held more than 1 year = long-term; 1 year or less = short-term (taxed as ordinary income). Loss on sale of personal-use property (your home) is not deductible. Unrecaptured §1250 gain max 25%.
  - https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc409

## Depreciation (cost recovery)
- Land is not depreciable. https://irs.gov/zh-hant/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/a-brief-overview-of-depreciation
- 26 USC 168: residential rental property 27.5 years, nonresidential real property 39 years, straight-line, mid-month convention; residential rental = 80%+ of gross rents from dwelling units. https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/26/168
- Course teaches simple annual = depreciable basis (building only) ÷ 27.5 or 39 (ignoring mid-month convention); say so.

## 1031 like-kind exchange
- Since 2018, only real property held for business or investment qualifies; property held primarily for sale (inventory) doesn't. Improved and unimproved real property are generally like-kind; U.S. vs foreign real property not like-kind. Boot (cash, debt relief, other property) makes gain taxable up to the boot. Losses not recognized. Form 8824.
  - https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/like-kind-exchanges-real-estate-tax-tips
- 45 days from transfer of relinquished property to identify in writing; receive replacement within 180 days or by return due date (with extensions), whichever earlier. Primary residences, second homes, vacation homes don't qualify. Use a qualified intermediary; taxpayer touching cash can blow the exchange; your own agent (including real estate agent/broker) within prior 2 years can't serve as QI. Basis carries over; tax-deferred, not tax-free.
  - https://www.irs.gov/node/5256 (2008 IRS fact sheet FS-2008-18, archival)

## Installment sale
- At least one payment received after the tax year of sale. Report gain proportionally as payments come in (Form 6252); can elect out. Not for losses or inventory; depreciation recapture portion reported in year of sale. Interest is ordinary income.
  - https://www.irs.gov/node/68456 (Topic 705)

## Mortgage interest / property tax deductions
- Mortgage interest deductible only if you itemize (Schedule A); debt secured by a qualified home (main or second home). Dollar limits exist and have changed by law (e.g., 2017 changes). Home equity interest deductible only if funds used to buy/build/substantially improve the home. Real estate taxes: separate itemized deduction subject to a state-and-local-tax cap (amount changes — not taught).
  - https://www.irs.gov/publications/p936

## Federal tax lien
- Arises after assessment, notice and demand, and failure to pay; attaches to all property, including after-acquired. Notice of Federal Tax Lien is a public filing alerting creditors.
  - https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/understanding-a-federal-tax-lien
- 26 USC 6323(b)(6): even after NFTL is filed, a local real property tax lien or special assessment lien that has priority under local law is not subordinated to the federal lien. https://govinfo.gov/link/uscode/26/6323

## Estate tax
- Tax on right to transfer property at death; gross estate at FMV; marital and charitable deductions; most estates don't file; threshold varies by year; Form 706. https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/estate-tax
- Inherited property basis generally = FMV at date of death. https://www.irs.gov/node/19461

## FIRPTA
- Covered in s4 (co-water-wells-taxes). Only a pointer here: buyer generally must withhold when seller is a foreign person; closing company usually handles. No rate given in this part.

## Appraisal Foundation / USPAP / AQB
- The Appraisal Foundation: Appraisal Standards Board writes/interprets USPAP; Appraiser Qualifications Board sets minimum education/experience/exam criteria. Both authorized by Congress under Title XI of FIRREA (1989). States license/certify appraisers.
  - https://appraisalfoundation.org/pages/boards
- AQB Real Property Appraiser Qualification Criteria (March 2026 version, hosted by Alabama REAB): Trainee (supervised by state-certified supervisory appraiser); Licensed Residential (non-complex 1–4 units < $1M, complex < $400k); Certified Residential (1–4 units regardless of value/complexity); Certified General (all types of real property). States may be stricter.
  - https://reab.alabama.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/20260325_RPAQC_Web_March-2026-1.pdf
  - Lesson avoids hour counts and the dollar thresholds (volatile).

## Appraisal independence
- Reg Z 12 CFR 1026.42: for consumer credit secured by principal dwelling, covered persons (creditors and settlement service providers incl. mortgage brokers, AMCs, real estate agents) may not coerce/influence the appraiser (e.g., demand a target value, withhold pay/future work based on value, condition pay on closing). Permitted: ask to consider additional info/comps, explain, correct errors. Must report material USPAP violations to state agency.
  - https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/42

## ARELLO
- Association of Real Estate License Law Officials: association supporting jurisdictions (regulators) that administer and enforce real estate license laws; mission to promote excellence in regulation to protect the public. Runs a license verification database (arello.com) and Distance Education Certification. It does not issue licenses.
  - https://www.arello.org/about/what-is-arello/

## Construction loans
- Standard principles (not jurisdiction-specific): short-term interim loan disbursed in draws/stages after inspection; interest on funds disbursed; often holdback/retainage; takeout (permanent) loan pays off construction loan; construction-to-permanent (single-close) combines them. Mechanics' lien risk motivates title updates/lien waivers per draw. Consistent with n2 (m2-loan-types) one-line mention.
