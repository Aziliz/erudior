# s4 research notes — Colorado closings & special topics (verified 2026-10-12)

## Closing responsibility
- C.R.S. 12-10-217(1)(j): ground for discipline = failing to provide purchaser and seller a closing statement containing info prescribed by Commission rules; failing to provide signed duplicate of listing contract and contract of sale. (1)(h) account/remit money in reasonable time; keep records. https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_12-10-217
- 4 CCR 725-1 Rule 6.19 (Closing Responsibility) https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/colorado/title-4/agency-725/division-1/chapter-6
  - A: broker with brokerage relationship responsible for proper closing; ensure consumer receives accurate, complete, detailed closing statement signed by the broker; deliver closing statements/docs to the firm immediately after closing.
  - B: broker who prepares and closes is responsible for accuracy & completeness.
  - C: broker must review closing docs and attend closing or be reasonably available; firm-designated substitute broker assumes joint responsibility for accuracy, completeness, delivery of signed statement.
  - D: earnest money per Rule 5.12.A.
  - E: per C.R.S. 38-35-125 (good funds) no disbursement until funds received and immediately withdrawable.
- Rule 6.14.A.2: duplicates of all documents broker prepares to all consumers at time prepared.
- Rule 5.12: earnest money transfer to closing entity with dated signed receipt. Rule 5.7: deposit deadlines 3 business days (5 for property management funds). Rule 5.5 separate accounts for rents and security deposits. Rule 5.15 beneficiary reports within 30 days after month end unless agreement says otherwise. https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/colorado/title-4/agency-725/division-1/chapter-5

## Closing Instructions (CL8, 2026) https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/Closing%20Instructions%20%28fillable%29_for%20use%20on%20or%20after%20January%201%2C%202026.pdf
- Signed by buyer, seller, closing company; brokers are not parties. Closing company prepares closing statements, records documents, disburses (incl. commissions) per contract; conflicts between instructions and contract -> Closing Instructions control (subject to amendments); conflicting party instructions require Agreement to Amend/Extend. Good funds before release. FIRPTA & Colorado withholding: closing co withholds from seller proceeds and remits to IRS / CO Dept of Revenue.
- Broker not responsible for reviewing/approving a deed the broker did not prepare.

## Documentary fee — C.R.S. 39-13-102 https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_39-13-102
- 1 cent per $100 of consideration (major fraction counts as $100); no fee if consideration $500 or less. Collected by county clerk and recorder at recording. Exemptions in 39-13-104. Declaration (TD-1000) under 39-14-102.
- Contract does not assign doc fee; customarily paid by buyer (custom—verify below).

## CBS1 2026 (https://dre.colorado.gov/...Residential V2 2026.pdf)
- 8.1: if neither box checked, Seller selects title company and pays owner's policy (8.1.1). OEC optional; premium allocation negotiable. Tax certificate payer negotiable.
- 8.4: Special taxing / metropolitan district disclosure (caps): districts may issue debt, impose mill levies, may increase costs; buyer should investigate (county treasurer, Certificate of Taxes Due); website of metro district blank.
- 15.1 each pays own closing costs in good funds. 15.2 closing services fee: buyer/seller/split/other. 15.3 Seller requests association status letter at least 14 days before closing; status letter fee paid by seller. 15.9 FIRPTA & Colorado withholding authorized from seller's proceeds (CO DOR withholding if seller will not be a CO resident after closing, unless exempt).
- 16.1 prorations to Closing Date; taxes default basis = calendar year immediately preceding Closing, most recent mill levy & most recent assessed/actual valuation; rents actually received or accrued (checkbox); security deposits transferred/credited, seller notifies tenants in writing. 16.2 association assessments: prepaid regular assessments credited to seller; reserves not credited to seller unless governing docs say otherwise.
- 2.7.3 well rights: for Small Capacity Well / Domestic Exempt Water Well used for household purposes, Buyer must complete a Change in Ownership form at or before Closing (contract wording; statute check below). 10.8: buyer acknowledges Seller's Property Disclosure or Source of Water Addendum; "SOME WATER PROVIDERS RELY ... ON NONRENEWABLE GROUND WATER."

## Wells — C.R.S. 38-30-102(3) (amended HB23-1125, eff. Aug 7 2023) https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_38-30-102 ; https://leg.colorado.gov/bills/hb23-1125
- Buyer of residential real estate must complete a change in owner name form for a transferred small capacity well or domestic exempt well used for household purposes (registration form if well unregistered, within 63 days after closing).
- Closing service provider submits the change-in-owner-name form to the Division of Water Resources within 60 days after closing (with as much info as available). If no closing service, buyer files within 60 days and pays fee. HB23-1125 removed requirement that buyer submit before closing. (Contract 2.7.3 says buyer completes form at or before closing.)

## Landlord-tenant
- Security deposit cap: C.R.S. 38-12-102.5 (eff. Aug 7 2023) — no more than two months' rent. https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_38-12-102.5
- Return: C.R.S. 38-12-103 — within one month after termination of lease or surrender and acceptance (whichever last), unless lease specifies longer, max 60 days. Written statement listing exact reasons for any retention; failure = forfeiture of right to withhold. No retention for normal wear and tear. Willful retention -> treble damages (+ fees); tenant gives 7 days' notice before suit; landlord bears burden of proof. https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_38-12-103
- Notice periods C.R.S. 13-40-104: nonpayment of rent 10 days' written demand (pay or possession); lease violation 10 days (comply or possession); 5 days for exempt residential agreement (landlord owns 5 or fewer single-family rental homes and lease says so); 3 days nonresidential/employer-provided housing. https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_13-40-104
- Warranty of habitability C.R.S. 38-12-503: implied in every residential rental agreement; tenant gives written/electronic notice; landlord responds within 24 hours; remedial action within 24 hours for conditions materially interfering with life/health/safety, 96 hours for other uninhabitable conditions (if tenant gave permission to enter); mold 96-hour containment; waivers void; tenant-caused conditions excluded. https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_38-12-503
- For-cause eviction HB24-1098 (signed Apr 19 2024; C.R.S. 38-12-1301 et seq.): landlord needs cause to evict or not renew after tenant has occupied ~1 year; no-fault grounds (sale? demolition, substantial renovation, family move-in, etc.) require 90 days' notice; relocation assistance in some no-fault cases (news summary unclear) — taught only at high level. Source: https://coloradonewsline.com/?p=34144 (secondary)

## Colorado Fair Housing — C.R.S. 24-34-502 (DRE 2025 compilation) https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/2025%20Colorado%20Fair%20Housing%20%2824-34-401%20et%20seq%29.pdf ; CCRD https://ccrd.colorado.gov/housing-discrimination
- (1)(a)(I): disability, race, creed, color, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression, marital status, familial status, veteran or military status, religion, national origin, ancestry. Source of income in separate subsections (1)(l)-(p),(r); source of income provisions don't apply to landlords with 3 or fewer units (1.5)(a).
- CCRD page: source of income eff. Jan 1 2021; veteran/military status eff. Aug 10 2022.
- Charge must be filed with CCRD within one year after alleged practice (24-34-504(1)). Private civil action 2 years.
- Beyond federal (race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, disability): creed, sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression, marital status, ancestry, veteran/military status, source of income.

## Foreclosure — C.R.S. 38-38
- Sale date: 110–125 calendar days after recording notice of election and demand (non-agricultural); 215–230 agricultural (38-38-108). https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_38-38-108
- Cure (38-38-104): owner (and others e.g. junior lienholders, guarantors) file notice of intent to cure no later than 15 calendar days before sale; pay cure amount by 12 noon the day before sale. https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_38-38-104
- Redemption (38-38-302): only junior lienors redeem (owner has no post-sale redemption; Pueblo County public trustee page states owner no longer has right to redeem after sale). Intent to redeem within 8 business days after sale; first redemption period 15–19 business days after sale. https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_38-38-302 ; https://pueblocounty.gov/treasurers-office/foreclosure-process
- Combined notice published 5 consecutive weeks; certificate of purchase to highest bidder; confirmation deed issued after redemption periods expire (no earlier than 15 business days after sale per Pueblo page). Lender attorney submits bid 2 business days before sale.

## Foreclosure Protection Act — C.R.S. 6-1-1101 et seq.
- 6-1-1102 purpose: protect distressed homeowners from deceptive/unfair schemes stripping equity; require info, minimum contract terms, cancellation rights; liberal construction. https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_6-1-1102
- 6-1-1103 defs: equity purchaser = acquires title to a residence in foreclosure, except one buying as personal residence; excludes deed in lieu to prior lienholder, trustee/sheriff deed, court transfers, relatives, financial institutions, short sale with state-promulgated short sale addendum complying with 6-1-1121. Foreclosure consultant = offers for compensation to stop/postpone sale etc.; excludes licensed real estate broker while engaging in licensed activity, attorneys, lenders. Residence in foreclosure = owner's principal residence with residential mortgage loan at least 30 days delinquent/in default. https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_6-1-1103
- 6-1-1105 foreclosure consultant contract: homeowner may cancel at any time by written notice; repay funds within 60 days; right not conditioned on repayment. https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_6-1-1105
- 6-1-1107: consultant may not collect compensation until fully performed every service; no wage assignment/lien; no acquiring interest in homeowner's property; no power of attorney. https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_6-1-1107
- 6-1-1113: homeowner may cancel equity purchaser contract until 12 midnight of the third business day after signing, or 12 noon on the day before the foreclosure sale, whichever first. https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_6-1-1113
- 6-1-1117(3): equity purchaser returns documents within 10 days of cancellation notice.
- Commission forms: CBS1 Residential (CFPA) contract; CFPA Homeowner Warning Notice, Seller Warning, Notice of Cancellation (from s3 notes).

## CFPA — DRE advisory https://dre.colorado.gov/division-notifications/understanding-the-colorado-foreclosure-protection-act-0 ; worksheet https://dre.colorado.gov/cfpa-worksheet
- Brokers excluded from "foreclosure consultant" while doing licensed activity, but a broker who buys a residence in foreclosure as an investor can be an equity purchaser.
- Short sale using Commission-approved short sale addendum & complying with 6-1-1121 excluded from equity purchaser.
- CFPA contract (CBSF1) usable only if seller has no right to lease back, no right to repurchase, buyer not assuming loan; otherwise attorney must draft. Attach Seller Warning and Notice of Cancellation; Homeowner Warning Notice translated if seller's principal language isn't English (broker should not translate).
- Penalties: criminal (fines/jail) and deceptive trade practice remedies (treble damages for bad faith).
- Worksheet triggers: investor buyer, not short sale, residential, owner-occupied seller, buyer knows of 30+ day delinquency.

## Nonresident withholding — C.R.S. 39-22-604.5 https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_39-22-604.5
- Closing/title company withholds lesser of 2% of sales price or net proceeds when nonresident individual (address outside CO on 1099-S/disbursement) or foreign corp without permanent CO place of business; none if price ≤ $100,000; exemptions via affirmation (CO resident, principal residence, no tax due) and foreclosure/deed in lieu to lender.

## Special district disclosure — C.R.S. 38-35.7-101: seller's duty; bold statement in every residential contract re special taxing districts' general obligation debt and mill levy risk; buyer should investigate (county treasurer certificate of taxes due). 38-35.7-111: from Jan 1 2024 seller of residential property in metro district organized on/after Jan 1 2000 provides official district website on the Commission-approved Seller's Property Disclosure (or contemporaneous doc). https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_38-35.7-101 ; https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_38-35.7-111

## Property tax
- C.R.S. 39-10-102: taxes due & payable Jan 1 of year after levied (paid in arrears). https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_39-10-102
- C.R.S. 39-10-104.5: full by last day of April, or two halves: last day of February and June 15. https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_39-10-104.5
- DPT: actual value x assessment rate = assessed value; assessed value x mill levy (mills/1000) = tax. Real property revalued every odd-numbered year; classification by use on Jan 1; taxing authorities set mill levies annually; appeal to assessor -> county board of equalization -> BAA/district court/arbitration. https://dpt.colorado.gov/understanding-property-taxes-in-colorado

## Good funds — C.R.S. 38-35-125: no disbursement until funds received and available for immediate withdrawal (wire, cashier's/certified/teller's check); provider may advance up to $500 for incidentals. https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_38-35-125

## Commission Closing Statement form https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/Closing%20Statement%20%28fillable%29.pdf
- Debits/credits; lines incl. Documentary Fee, 2% Colorado Withholding, FIRPTA, Rents proration, Security Deposits; signature lines for buyer/seller and Broker.

## Water rights
- C.R.S. 38-30-102(2): water rights conveyed with same formalities as real estate (except where ditch company stock is the water right). 
- LTGC article: water right is real property, can be severed and conveyed separately by deed recorded with county clerk; may pass as appurtenance if deed silent (intent question); ditch shares by stock assignment. https://www.ltgc.com/articles/water-law2/
- CSU Extension 6.700: prior appropriation "first in time, first in right"; exempt wells (household use only on <35 ac: indoor only; domestic/livestock on 35+ ac: up to 3 homes, 1 acre lawn/garden, livestock; generally 15 gpm); non-exempt wells require augmentation plan approved by water court replacing depletions. https://www.extension.colostate.edu/docs/pubs/natres/06700.pdf

## Licensing for property management — C.R.S. 12-10-201(6): broker includes renting/leasing for compensation, including employee of owner; exemption for regularly salaried on-site manager of apartment/condo owner for customary duties. https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_12-10-201
