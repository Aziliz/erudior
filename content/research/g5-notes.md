## Position statements (CREM Ch. 3) — https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/2026_CREM_Ch%2003.pdf
- Intro: CPs "are not law"; "non-binding direction"; read as a series of best practices. 31 CPs listed (CP-1 ... CP-31), e.g. CP-10 manufactured homes, CP-11 sale of items other than real estate, CP-14 broker buying property, CP-15 leasing/PM, CP-16 TB in particular transactions, CP-22 conflicts, CP-28 minimum service requirements, CP-29 holding money for non-brokerage activities.
- CP-15: brokers must be "worthy and competent"; a broker lacking competency in leasing/PM must address it under the competency rule before providing services; employing broker must be competent to supervise. CP-10: manufactured home deals differ; ensure competency first.
- CP-16 (via CREM summary): team member buying a team listing — written informed consent of both parties, remove buying broker from listing, firewall.

## CP-29 non-brokerage money — https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/Commission%20Position%2029%20-%20Holding%20Money%20Belonging%20to%20Others%20for%20Activities%20Not%20Involving%20Real%20Estate%20Brokerage%20Services.pdf
- Commission has jurisdiction; Seibel v. CREC (Colo. App. 1974); McDonnell v. CREC (Colo. App. 2015); statute 12-10-217(1)(h) "whether acting as real estate brokers or otherwise".
- Examples: security deposits for broker-owned rentals, short-term rental deposits, deposits as a builder, fundraising for child's sports team, deposits for painting work.
- Must be in trust/escrow account, not commingled, records kept, Ch. 5 accounting.
- First check Office Policy Manual; if firm requires firm accounts -> firm trust account, three-way reconciliation as usual; otherwise broker opens own separate trust/escrow account, keeps journal, produces records on request, monthly TWO-way reconciliation (journal = reconciled bank balance).
- No specific client disclosure requirement in CP-29. (Rule 5.11 confirmed in CREM Ch. 2: short-term rental guest deposits etc. go in trust/escrow.)

## CP-22 conflicts (rev. 10/7/25) — https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/Commission%20Position%2022%20-%20Conflicts%20of%20Interest%20revised%2010.7.25.pdf
- Continuing duty to disclose in writing (Rule 6.17). Examples: PM owning/financial/family tie to vendor (disclose early, e.g. management agreement, offer opt-out); affiliated business arrangements; broker as principal/20%+ owner of entity; TB with close relationship to a party; referral fees for non-settlement services (security, internet, moving, contractors) must be disclosed; settlement-service referral fees -> RESPA counsel; employing broker supervising both sides with confidential info; employing broker personally on other side. Perceived conflicts: err toward disclosure.

## CP-14 broker buying property — https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/Commission%20Position%2014%20-%20Broker%20Buying%20Property.pdf
- Principal still under Commission jurisdiction (Seibel 1974). Disclose license status in writing (Rule 6.17.B), in the contract; use Brokerage Disclosure, treat other party as Customer. Buying own listing strongly discouraged: terminate/amend listing, written disclosure acting as principal with prior confidential access, license in contract, seller option to terminate through closing. Guaranteed buy-out programs: check OPM; Commission-approved Licensee Buy-Out Addendum; keep property marketed as active.
- DRE clarification (Spring 2023 newsletter) — broker-owned managed rentals: disclose in writing ownership + licensed broker status; in the lease is sufficient. https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/Clarification%20of%20Spring%202023%20Newsletter.pdf

## CP-28 minimum service requirements — https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/Commission%20Position%2028%20-%20Minimum%20Service%20Requirements.pdf
- Broker acts as TB or single agent; statutory uniform duties are mandatory; "a Broker may not waive these duties, in writing or otherwise"; CREC v. Vizzi (Colo. App. 2019). Additional duties in writing. Commission does not regulate fees/commissions; negotiable.

## Rules (CREM Ch. 2) — https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/2026_CREM_Ch%2002.pdf
- 6.2 competency: necessary experience, training, knowledge; keep up with laws; if lacking: decline, acquire, get help from qualified supervising broker or legal counsel, or co-list with qualified broker.
- 1.52 Team: 2+ brokers within a brokerage firm cooperating on ongoing basis. 6.10.B: team name can't use Realty, Real estate, Realtors, Company, Corporation, entity suffixes or imply separate entity; all team ads clearly and conspicuously include firm legal or trade name; firm provides team member names on request of consumer, Commission, other firm/broker; no outside-firm use of team name.
- 1.21 Duplicate includes photocopy, fax, or electronic copy containing a digital or electronic signature.
- 5.7.B earnest money deposited within 3 business days after receipt or mutual execution, whichever later; 5.7.A PM funds 5 business days.
- 5.9 conversion; 5.10 commingling; 5.14 monthly 3-way reconciliation; no negative ledger; 5.21 produce records by Commission deadline or grounds for discipline.
- 6.3.A.1: complaint against associate -> Commission may also investigate supervising employing broker.

## UETA — https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_24-71.3-107
- Electronic record/signature not denied legal effect solely because electronic; electronic record satisfies writing requirement; electronic signature satisfies signature requirement.

## DRE audit process (2023-01-31) — https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/2023-01-31%20DRE-Audit%20Process.pdf
- Selection: random/routine; stipulated (follow-up audits after discipline); complaint-based.
- Types: PM & Practice; Sales Only; Non-Real Estate Brokerage Services (associate brokers managing own properties incl. STRs).
- Notification of Audit Letter; respond within 15 calendar days (affirmation & questionnaire for past 12 months, last 3 closed sales files, latest 3-way reconciliations, 3 PM files, Notice of Trust or Escrow signed by bank, checklists). Correction letter -> 10 calendar days. Close with Audit Closure Letter or report to Commission. "Final Notice to Comply" if no response. (Deadlines not used in lesson — internal guidance, may change.)
- Common deficiencies: no monthly 3-way reconciliation; negative owner ledgers; commingling / broker "cushion"; account name lacks trust/escrow + purpose; broker-owned rental security deposits not in trust account; missing broker disclosures / brokerage duties addendum to PMAs; failing to disclose license status and conflicts in writing.
- Commission outcomes: remedial education, fines, public censure, follow-up audits, practice restrictions, probation, suspension, revocation.

## Complaint process — https://dre.colorado.gov/complaint-process ; https://dre.colorado.gov/division-notifications/licensee-advisory-what-happens-if-a-complaint-is-filed-against-my-license
- Anyone may file (online preferred, mail); anonymous accepted (no updates to anonymous complainants). Jurisdiction review (outside jurisdiction -> dismissed; e.g. rudeness); licensee not told of dismissed non-jurisdictional complaint. Assigned to investigator (neutral fact finder); respondent notified, usually gets copy; must respond; failure may lead to discipline. Investigation goal ~240 days. No violation -> dismissed; otherwise findings to Commission which decides violation/action. Discipline -> Expedited Settlement Program (goal 90 days) or Attorney General litigation -> Office of Administrative Courts, ALJ (may take 18+ months). Investigations closed to public. Division can't enforce/interpret contracts (civil matters), no HOA authority; investigates individuals. Advice: engage early, consider attorney, contact E&O carrier. Delay/lying/altering documents can affect discipline.

## Statutes
- 12-10-217 (https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_12-10-217): investigate on own motion; must investigate on written complaint; after hearing: fine ≤ $2,500 per separate offense, censure, probation, temporary suspension, permanent revocation; firm's right to operate; (1)(h) account for money "whether acting as real estate brokers or otherwise", records commission can audit; (7) letter of admonition by certified mail, request formal proceedings in writing within 20 days after proven receipt -> letter vacated; (6) complaints/investigation files closed, stipulations and final agency orders public; (9) revoked -> no application for 1 year.
- 12-10-219 (https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_12-10-219): ALJ conducts hearings per 24-4-104/105; Commission decides by majority vote; review by Court of Appeals; court may stay order.
- 12-10-409: relationship ends at earliest of agreed expiration, termination, or one year after engagement if not completed; afterward account for money and keep confidential info (s2 notes).
- Recovery fund: repealed by 2005 Colo. Sess. Laws ch. 177, "Concerning the Repeal of the Real Estate Recovery Fund" (https://scholar.law.colorado.edu/session-laws-2001-2050/2077). No dollar amounts used.

## Federal
- 26 U.S.C. 3508 (https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/3508): qualified real estate agent = licensed; substantially all pay tied to sales/output not hours; written contract saying not treated as employee for federal tax -> not an employee for federal employment tax.
