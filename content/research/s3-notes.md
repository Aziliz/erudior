## Forms page — https://dre.colorado.gov/real-estate-broker-contracts-and-forms
- "Applicable law requires real estate brokers to use Commission approved contracts and forms" as appropriate to a transaction or circumstance.
- Broker who is a party to a transaction may have counsel prepare a form so long as it conspicuously states it is not a Commission-approved form. Page references attorney-prepared forms "as provided in Rule 7.1".
- Current sales contracts (mandatory use on/after Jan 1, 2026): Residential; Residential (Colorado Foreclosure Protection Act); Income-Residential; Commercial; Land; Manufactured Home Contract to Buy and Sell (Lot Lease Only).
- NO Farm & Ranch contract currently listed on the page (CBS5 not offered) -> do not teach a current farm & ranch form.
- Listing contracts (2026): Exclusive Right to Sell; Exclusive Right to Buy; Exclusive Tenant; Exclusive Right to Lease.
- Counterproposal (for use after Aug 14, 2024). Agreement to Amend/Extend Contract (after Aug 14, 2024). Agreement to Revive Contract (2026). Extension or Termination of Contract (2026).
- Notices: Notice to Terminate; Inspection Objection Notice; Inspection Resolution; Title-Related Objection Notice; Appraised Value Objection Notice; CFPA Homeowner Warning Notice, Seller Warning, Notice of Cancellation; Seller Authorization; Seller Association Authorization.
- Closing forms: Earnest Money Receipt; Earnest Money Release (with Mutual Release); Closing Statement; Closing Instructions.
- Disclosures: Seller's Property Disclosure (Residential / Commercial / Land / Supplement-Additional Structure); Square Footage Disclosure; Lead-Based Paint Disclosures (Sales/Rentals) and Obligations of Seller/Landlord; Green Disclosure; Estoppel Statement; Dual Status Disclosure; Definitions of Working Relationships; Change of Status; Brokerage Disclosures to Buyer/Seller(FSBO)/Tenant/Landlord.
- Addenda: Residential Addendum; Source of Water Addendum; Short Sale Addendum (Seller Listing Contract) and (Contract to Buy & Sell); Open Listing Addendum; Exclusive Brokerage Listing Addendum; Exchange Addendum; Licensee Buy-Out Addendum; Manufactured Home Addendum.
- Promissory notes: Earnest Money Promissory Note; Promissory Note for Deed of Trust; Promissory Note (UCCC-No Default Rate).
- Deeds of trust: Due on Transfer-Creditworthy; Due on Transfer-Strict; Assumable-Not Due on Transfer.
- Deeds: Warranty, Special Warranty, Bargain and Sale, Quit Claim. Bills of sale. Optional: Post-Closing Occupancy Agreement, Personal Property Agreement, TD-1000.
- Deed forms may need adjustment for local recording — check with county Clerk and Recorder.
- 2027 versions posted "for informational purposes" (use on/after Jan 1, 2027) — shows forms are revised periodically with a mandatory-use date.

## CBS1 (Residential) — for use on/after Jan 1, 2026
https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/Contract%20to%20Buy%20and%20Sell%20Real%20Estate%20%28Residential%29%20V2%20%28fillable%29_for%20use%20on%20or%20after%20January%201%2C%202026.pdf
- Sections: 1 Agreement; 2 Parties & Property (inclusions, exclusions, water rights); 3 Dates, Deadlines & Applicability; 4 Purchase Price & Terms; 5 Financing Conditions; 6 Appraisal; 7 Owners' Associations; 8 Title Insurance, Record Title, Off-Record Title; 9 New ILC/New Survey; 10 Property Disclosure, Inspection, Indemnity, Insurability, Due Diligence, Source of Water; 11 (deleted); 12 Closing docs/instructions/closing; 13 Transfer of Title; 14 Liens; 15 Closing costs, association status letter, taxes/withholding; 16 Prorations; 17 Possession; 18 Causes of loss/insurance/walk-through; 19 Legal & tax counsel; 20 Time of essence, default & remedies; 21 Legal fees; 22 Mediation; 23 Earnest money dispute; 24 Termination; 25 Entire agreement; 26 Notice, delivery, choice of law; 27 Notice of acceptance, counterparts; 28 Good faith; 29 Buyer's brokerage firm compensation; 30 Additional Provisions; 31 Other documents.
- Deadlines: "day" ends 11:59 p.m. Mountain Time. Optional Time of Day Deadline applies to objection/resolution/examination/termination deadlines; blank/N/A -> 11:59 p.m. MT.
- Counting periods: exclude first day, include last day. MEC = mutual execution of contract.
- Weekend/holiday: "Will / Will Not" checkbox decides whether a deadline on Sat/Sun/federal or Colorado holiday extends to next business day; if neither box checked, it does NOT extend.
- Deadline blank / N/A / "Deleted" -> that deadline and related provision do not apply.
- Time is of the essence.
- Acceptance: offer expires unless signed by both and offering party receives notice of acceptance by Acceptance Deadline Date and Time.
- Earnest money: holder holds in trust for both parties; due with contract unless Alternative Earnest Money Deadline; may be released to closing company at/before closing; on termination party entitled must sign Earnest Money Release within 3 days of receipt or be liable under default provisions.
- Title: if neither box checked, Seller selects title insurance company. Buyer objects by Record Title Objection Deadline; late docs/new exceptions -> earlier of Closing or 10 days after receipt. No objection = accepts title.
- Off-record: seller delivers existing surveys and discloses known off-record matters by Off-Record Title Deadline; buyer may inspect; object by Off-Record Title Objection Deadline.
- Title objection unresolved in writing by Title Resolution Deadline -> contract terminates unless buyer withdraws objection in writing before deadline.
- Association documents: seller provides at seller's expense by Association Documents Deadline; buyer may terminate by Association Documents Termination Deadline; late docs -> 10 days after receipt.
- Seller's Property Disclosure: current Commission form by deadline; continuing duty to disclose newly discovered adverse material facts; buyer may terminate within earlier of Closing or 5 days after new disclosure.
- Inspection: Inspection Termination Deadline (terminate if no objection delivered); Inspection Objection Deadline (written list); unresolved by Inspection Resolution Deadline -> contract terminates unless buyer withdraws objection. Buyer pays inspections, repairs damage, indemnifies seller.
- Appraisal: appraised value < price or not received by Appraisal Deadline -> buyer may terminate or object by Appraisal Objection Deadline (objection includes appraisal or lender verification); unresolved -> terminates at Appraisal Resolution Deadline unless withdrawn. FHA/VA have own language; appraisal deadlines don't apply to FHA/VA.
- Loan: New Loan Application, New Loan Terms (sole discretion), New Loan Availability deadlines. Failure to timely terminate -> earnest money nonrefundable except as otherwise provided.
- Deed: if no deed type selected, special warranty deed.
- Possession: on Possession Date/Time; holdover seller subject to eviction and per-day amount + actual damages.
- Prorations: to Closing Date; default basis = taxes for calendar year immediately preceding closing, most recent mill levy and assessed valuation. Rents based on rents actually received. Prorations final unless additional provisions say otherwise.
- Default: Buyer default — if specific performance box checked, seller may cancel & keep EM or enforce specific performance/damages; if not checked, liquidated damages apply (EM is seller's sole remedy, except indemnity & legal fees sections). Seller default — buyer may cancel, get EM back and seek damages, or seek specific performance and/or damages.
- Legal fees: prevailing party recovers reasonable costs and attorney fees.
- Mediation: required before arbitration/litigation; jointly selected mediator; costs split equally; obligation ends if not resolved within 30 days of request. Lis pendens may still be recorded.
- EM dispute: holder may hold, interplead, or return to buyer if no suit filed within 120 days of notice.
- Termination: effective when other party receives written Notice to Terminate by deadline; late notice ineffective and right waived; EM returned to buyer.
- Notice: in writing, effective on receipt by party or broker/brokerage firm (after closing, party itself). Electronic notice via email/fax/other listed; electronic delivery by email, link/website, fax. Colorado law governs.

## 4 CCR 725-1 Chapter 7 (Use of Standard Forms) — https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/colorado/title-4/agency-725/division-1/chapter-7
- Rule 7.1: under C.R.S. 12-10-403(4), broker may complete Standard Forms when acting as single agent or transaction-broker. Broker must advise parties forms have important legal consequences and to consult legal counsel before signing.
- Categories: A Commission-approved (must be used when one exists and is appropriate); B Attorney Form (only if no appropriate CA form; disclaimer "This form has not been approved by the Colorado Real Estate Commission" + drafting attorney/firm name + broker/firm it was prepared for; broker may only fill blanks); C Client Form (provided by a party; broker keeps written confirmation of who provided it; insert transaction-specific info only); D Government/Lender form; E Colorado Bar Association form (only if no CA form); F Disclosure form (can't create/waive rights; disclaimer); G Title Company form; H Letter of Intent (nonbinding statement + disclaimer).
- Rule 7.2: may add firm identifying info, initial lines, lengthen/shorten blanks; delete printed text only as result of negotiation or party instruction, struck through legibly (text still readable); inserted provisions in distinguishable font; inapplicable sections deleted but caption kept with "omitted-not applicable"; must explain all changes/addenda to principal; electronic forms protected against changes.
- Rule 7.3: additional provisions that modify the form must result from negotiations/party instructions. Attorney-drafted transaction-specific clause: broker must understand it; keep for 4 years from last use with attorney's name; provide to Commission on request.
- Rule 7.4: no provision relieves broker of license law; broker not a principal may not insert exculpatory language/disclaimers in own favor; may include commission-payment language at principal's direction if negotiated.
- Rule 7.5: when principal (builder, bank/REO, iBuyer) requires its own contract, broker representing consumer must advise in writing to seek legal advice before signing.

## Additional verified facts (2026-10-12, second pass)
- C.R.S. 12-10-403(4) — https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_12-10-403 : broker (single agent or transaction-broker) may complete standard forms; 8 categories (commission-approved; attorney-drafted for broker/firm with names; party-provided (keep written confirmation of provider); govt/lender-required; Colorado Bar Association-approved; disclosure-only forms; title-company closing forms; nonbinding letters of intent). MUST use commission-approved form when one exists and is appropriate. Party/lender forms: insert transaction-specific info only. Must advise forms have important legal consequences and parties should consult legal counsel before signing.
- Conway-Bogue Realty Inv. Co. v. Denver Bar Ass'n, 135 Colo. 398, 312 P.2d 998 (1957) — summarized in Colorado Real Estate Manual (2007 ed.) https://spl.cde.state.co.us/artemis/regserials/reg52908r22internet/2007%20manual/reg52908r22200707internet.pdf : filling in standard forms (receipts, options, deeds, notes, deeds of trust) is practice of law but court declined to enjoin brokers; brokers may fill blanks on standard forms only in transactions where acting as broker, with no compensation beyond ordinary commission; not for others as favor; no drafting beyond customary; seek attorney when legal complications arise.
- Listing contract (ERTS, LC50, 2026) — §5.1.2 present all offers even if under contract; §5.4 not obligated to seek more offers while under contract; §5.7 seller Does/Does Not consent to disclose existence of offers; neither checked = no consent; disclosing terms requires seller's written consent. §7.2 compensation earned: sale during listing period by anyone; broker finds ready, willing, able buyer; holdover sale to Submitted Prospect (names submitted in writing during listing period). Holdover Will/Will Not; no compensation owed if seller signed a new exclusive with another firm during holdover and that firm earns it. §3.9.2 weekend/holiday Will/Will Not (neither = not extended). §27 mediation 30 days; §28 attorney fees. §18.2.3 CO alarm; §18.2.2 lead paint pre-1978. §12 firm may accept earnest money and deliver to closing agent.
- ERTB (BC60, 2026): Buyer Agency or Transaction-Brokerage; neither box checked = transaction-broker. Compensation negotiable/not set by law; seller-paid amounts credited; buyer pays unpaid portion only if disclosed in writing before buyer contracts. Holdover: property submitted in writing during listing period. Broker may show same property to other buyers (§5.3). Wire fraud warning.
- DRE notice "Refusing to Present Offers to Sellers" https://dre.colorado.gov/division-notifications/refusing-to-present-offers-to-sellers : 12-10-404 seller agent must present all offers timely even if under contract; can't refuse because buyer broker doesn't use particular software; any limitation on offers must be discussed with seller and memorialized in writing in listing contract; if listing broker unresponsive, buyer's broker goes to employing broker, then seller directly.
- CBS1 extra: §2.5.1 attached items included; §2.5.6 personal property by bill of sale; §4.2 seller concession; §5.2.2 New Loan Availability; §5.3 seller may evaluate buyer credit info; §9 ILC/survey ordered/paid per checkbox; §10.7 conditional sale; §10.10 lead-based paint (built/permit before 1/1/1978); §10.11 CO alarm within 15 ft of bedroom entrances; §10.12 meth; §18.1 damage >10% of price -> buyer may terminate; §15.3 status letter requested ≥14 days before closing, seller pays status letter fee; title default: seller selects and pays owner's policy (§8.1.1); OEC optional; appraisal deadlines N/A to FHA/VA; §4.3.2 EM release signed within 3 days; failure = default. Notice to broker = notice to party except after closing/termination per §26.
- Counterproposal CP40-8-24: supersedes prior counterproposal; terms not changed remain the same; expires if notice of acceptance not received by deadline; originating party should not sign the Contract itself.
- Amend/Extend AE41-8-24: signed by both; all other terms remain same; expires without acceptance by deadline.
- Agreement to Revive Contract (AR43, mandatory 1/1/2026): revive terminated contract; seller represents no other contract in effect.
- Extension or Termination of Contract (ET35, 2026): Part I extension; if not accepted, Part II serves as notice to terminate.
- Inspection Objection Notice: from buyer; unresolved by Inspection Resolution Deadline -> terminates unless buyer withdraws. Inspection Resolution (NTC43R): signed by buyer and seller, amends contract, seller performs at seller's expense.
- Earnest Money Release EMR83: signed by buyer & seller (no broker signature); mutual release.
- Seller's Property Disclosure (Residential) SPD19 (2026): seller completes, not broker; current actual knowledge; not a warranty; no duty to inspect.
- Square Footage Disclosure SF94 (Mandatory 1-19): listing licensee completes; for marketing only; sources (licensee measurement & standard, appraisal, plans, assessor, other); buyer should measure independently by deadline.
- Source of Water Addendum SWA35-8-10: well permit #, water provider, or other source; nonrenewable groundwater note.
- Lead-Based Paint (Sales) LP45: pre-1978; pamphlet; 10-day risk assessment opportunity (waivable/modifiable in writing); licensees ensure compliance; seller keeps copy 3 years.
- Short Sale Addendum (CBS): Short Sale Acceptance Deadline — no acceptance by then -> contract terminates; seller can terminate within 3 days after acceptance if terms unacceptable; seller may remain liable for deficiency unless released; tax/1099 warnings; deadline changes need Amend/Extend.
- Residential CFPA contract: applies when residential, in foreclosure (or buyer has notice of 30+ day delinquency), seller occupies as principal residence, buyer won't occupy. Conditions: no assumption of seller obligations, no leaseback, no repurchase option, Notice of Cancellation & Seller Warning attached, no consideration before cancellation right expires. C.R.S. 6-1-1113(1) (https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_6-1-1113): homeowner may cancel until earlier of midnight of 3rd business day after signing or noon day before foreclosure sale. CFPA doesn't apply if Short Sale Addendum part of contract.
- Closing Instructions (2026): signed by buyer, seller, closing company; closing company prepares statutory deeds (special warranty, general warranty, bargain & sale, quitclaim); PR/trustee deeds or nonstatutory exceptions come from parties; good funds; FIRPTA & Colorado withholding; no wiring on email instructions alone; changes must be in writing signed by all three.
- Promissory Note NTD81 + Deeds of Trust (TD73 creditworthy; strict; assumable). Deed of trust names Public Trustee as trustee; due-on-transfer-creditworthy: lender may accelerate if transferee not creditworthy. Original note needed to release deed of trust via Public Trustee. Forms caution consult counsel if consumer credit transaction.
- CBS2 Income-Residential (1-4 units / larger), CBS3 Commercial, CBS4 Land (no residences or with Residential Addendum) — all mandatory 1/1/2026. Commercial: Phase I environmental, ADA, estoppels. Land: growing crops, water, mineral rights.
- CREM Ch.3 CP-14 (https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/2026_CREM_Ch%2003.pdf): broker buying/selling as principal must use Commission-approved form when one exists; disclose licensed status in writing. CP-15: no Commission-approved leases or management agreements.
