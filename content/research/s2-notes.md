# s2 research notes — Colorado brokerage relationships, trust accounts, records (verified 2026-10-12)

Bash egress to dre.colorado.gov blocked (proxy 403); facts gathered via WebFetch of official pages/PDFs and colorado.public.law (statute text, citing OLLS 2024 C.R.S.). Rule numbers below are from the current 4 CCR 725-1 as shown on LII (law.cornell.edu) and DRE pages; the lesson text avoids rule numbers per SPEC.

## C.R.S. 12-10-402 definitions — https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_12-10-402
- Customer: party with no brokerage relationship because party has not engaged/employed the broker.
- Designated broker: employing or employed broker designated IN WRITING by employing broker as single agent or transaction-broker; excludes a firm of only one licensed natural person.
- Single agent = seller's, buyer's, landlord's, tenant's agent (represents only one party).
- Limited agent: duties only those in 404/405 plus any agreed under 403(5).
- Transaction-broker: assists one or more parties without being agent/advocate for any party; may become single agent by written agreement or written disclosure.
- Dual agent defined in statute, but 12-10-406: broker shall NOT act as dual agent (https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_12-10-406).

## 12-10-403 — https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_12-10-403
- (2) Broker PRESUMED transaction-broker unless single agency established by written agreement.
- (3) Same person may be represented under different relationships in different transactions.
- (4) Standard forms; must use Commission-approved form when one exists; advise parties of legal consequences / consult counsel.
- (5) Parties may contract for additional duties in writing.
- (6) Designated brokerage: relationship does not extend to employing broker, other brokers, or firm; designated brokers in same firm may be single agents for opposite parties without dual agency for employing broker; one individual broker may be TB for both, or single agent for one + other as customer, but NOT single agent for both; knowledge of designated single agent not imputed to non-designated brokers; must be disclosed.
- (7) Principals not vicariously liable for broker acts they did not approve, direct or ratify.
- (8) Doesn't limit employing broker's supervision duty or vicarious liability.

## 12-10-404 seller's/landlord's agent — https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_12-10-404
- (1) Duties: perform written agreement; reasonable skill and care; promote interests with utmost good faith, loyalty, fidelity incl.: seek acceptable price/terms (no duty to seek other offers while under contract/leased/LOI); present all offers to and from timely even if under contract; disclose adverse material facts actually known; counsel on material benefits/risks actually known; advise to obtain expert advice; account for money/property timely; inform not vicariously liable. Comply with article 10 & rules, fair housing/civil rights laws.
- (2) Not disclose without informed consent: willing to accept less; motivations; accept other financing terms; material info about seller unless required by law or failure = fraud/dishonest dealing; psychologically impacting facts (38-35.5-101).
- (3) No duties to buyer except disclose adverse material facts actually known (title, physical condition, material defects, environmental hazards required by law). No duty to independently inspect for buyer or verify statements of seller/inspectors.
- (4) May show other properties, list competing properties.
- (5) May cooperate with other brokers but shall not engage/create subagents.

## 12-10-405 buyer's/tenant's agent — https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_12-10-405
- Same duty structure; no duty to seek other properties while buyer under contract.
- (2) Not disclose: buyer willing to pay more; motivations; accept other financing; material info about buyer; psychological impact facts.
- (3) Duty to seller only: disclose adverse material facts actually known incl. buyer's financial ability to perform and intent to occupy as principal residence. No duty to conduct independent investigation of buyer's financial condition or verify buyer statements.
- (4) May show properties of interest to other buyers; may show same property to competing buyers.
- (5) No duty to independently inspect or verify. (6) No subagents.

## 12-10-407 transaction-broker — https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_12-10-407
- Not agent for either party.
- Duties: perform terms of any written or ORAL agreement; reasonable skill and care incl.: present all offers/counteroffers timely even if under contract; advise parties, suggest expert advice; account promptly for money/property; keep parties fully informed; assist parties in complying with contract terms and closing; disclose to buyers adverse material facts actually known (title, condition, defects, environmental hazards); disclose to sellers adverse material facts actually known re buyer's financial ability and intent to occupy as principal residence; inform not vicariously liable. Comply with article 10 and fair housing/civil rights laws.
- (3) Not disclose without informed consent of ALL parties: buyer will pay more; seller accept less; motivations of any party; accept other financing; psychological impact facts; material info about other party.
- (4) No duty to inspect/verify; (5) no duty to investigate buyer finances.
- (6) May show other properties, list competing, show to other buyers, act as agent/TB in other transactions.
- (7) No imputed knowledge between broker and party or among persons within an entity acting as TB.
- (8) No subagents.

## 12-10-408 disclosures — https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_12-10-408
- (1) Written office policy identifying relationships offered; need not offer all; must inform parties other relationships exist; if party asks about relationship not offered -> provide Commission written definition.
- (2)(a) TB: before engaging in brokerage activities, disclose in writing not an agent; signature block for acknowledgment; not a contract; if party won't sign, note on copy and keep.
- (2)(b) Single agency: written agreement BEFORE engaging in activities, describing duties; timely notice to prospective parties.
- (2)(c) Seller's agent working with buyer: written disclosure before activities: is seller's agent not buyer's; list of tasks; buyer not vicariously liable; signature block.
- (2)(d) Disclose relationship to other potential parties at earliest reasonable opportunity.
- (2)(e) Written agreements must state relationship is only with designated broker.

## 12-10-409 duration — https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_12-10-409
- Begins at engagement, continues until performance/completion; if not completed, ends at earliest of agreed expiration, termination by parties, or one year after engagement.
- After end: account for money/property; keep confidential info confidential unless written consent, required by law, or made public by party/other source.

## 12-10-410 compensation — https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_12-10-410
- Compensation may come from seller, buyer, landlord, tenant, third party, or split; payment alone does not create agency.
- Buyer's agent needs buyer's WRITTEN approval before proposing to seller's agent that buyer's agent be paid from seller's compensation.
- Parties shall be informed of compensation arrangement BEFORE signing brokerage agreement or contract to buy/sell/lease.
- May be paid by more than one party if parties consent in writing before entering the contract.

## 38-35.5-101 psychologically impacted — https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_38-35.5-101
- Not material facts requiring disclosure: occupant had/suspected HIV/AIDS or other disease highly unlikely to be transmitted through occupancy; site of homicide, other felony, or suicide. No cause of action vs broker for failing to disclose.

## Commission Position 27 (adverse material facts)
https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/Commission%20Position%2027%20-%20Broker%20Disclosure%20of%20Adverse%20Material%20Facts.pdf
- Only facts actually known; not "should have known"; examples: structural, environmental/health hazards, zoning/building violations, liens, buyer financial issues; when unsure about physical property facts, err toward disclosure.

## Rules ch. 6 (LII https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/colorado/title-4/agency-725/division-1/chapter-6 ; DRE https://dre.colorado.gov/brokerage-relationship-disclosures)
- 6.4 written office policy manual; designations in writing; confidentiality safeguards; employing broker may supervise and also be designated broker.
- 6.5 written disclosure "prior to eliciting or discussing confidential information"; DRE page: "at the earliest reasonable opportunity". Small talk on price range, location, property styles, and general factual questions about advertised properties don't trigger.
- 6.6 associate broker may take listing as designated broker; teams: all members designated, same capacity, all named in listing contract; employing broker may substitute/add designated brokers and must notify consumer.
- 6.7 same broker/team both sides: cannot be single agent for one and SA or TB for the other; with written disclosure (e.g. listing contract) may be TB for both, TB+customer, or SA+customer.
- 6.8 ministerial tasks for customer: showing, scrivener, conveying offers, explaining financing, info about services — don't create relationship.
- 6.9 Change of Status form: when broker goes from SA for one to TB for both; given to the consumer whose relationship changes, "not later than at the time the Consumer signs the contract".
- Definitions of Working Relationships form (DD25-5-09): defines seller's agent, buyer's agent, TB, customer; "THIS IS NOT A CONTRACT"; per DRE page, first section required only when party asks about a relationship not offered — otherwise a reference.
  https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/Definitions%20of%20Working%20Relationships%20%28fillable%29.pdf
- 6.14 listing contracts (seller/landlord) must be in writing before brokerage services; definite termination date; holdover only to persons/properties negotiated and names submitted in writing during term; copies of documents to consumers when prepared.
- 6.17 disclose conflicts in writing; licensee buying/selling own account must disclose license status in contract or concurrent writing.
- 6.19 broker with relationship responsible for closing; signed closing statement.
- 6.20 transaction files kept 4 years from consummation or from listing expiration if no closing. Statute 12-10-217(1)(k) four years.
- 6.24 electronic records allowed; must be retrievable/legibly printable; produce on request of Commission or principal party.
- 6.25 written response to complaint/audit by deadline; failure = grounds for discipline.
- DRE https://dre.colorado.gov/transaction-file-requirements-and : 4 yrs; hard copy or electronic; broker still responsible for files after changing firms; Transaction File Checklist; texts/emails recommended not required.

## Rules ch. 5 (LII https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/colorado/title-4/agency-725/division-1/chapter-5 ; 2026 CREM Ch.2)
- 5.1 written accounting control policies.
- 5.2 money belonging to others separate, in trust/escrow accounts at Recognized Depository (FDIC/NCUA insured); deposit agreement must label "trust" or "escrow" + purpose (e.g. sales escrow, rental escrow, security deposit escrow); keep copy of deposit agreement.
- Statute 12-10-217(1)(i): account with bank/recognized depository "in this state", insured by US agency. https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_12-10-217
- 5.3 sole proprietor -> account in employing/independent broker's name; entity -> entity's name; employing broker must have withdrawal authority.
- 5.4 zero to unlimited accounts.
- 5.5 property management: separate accounts — at least one for rents, at least one for security deposits.
- 5.6 funds available immediately without penalty; no fixed-term/penalty accounts or CDs unless written agreement.
- 5.7 deposit: property management funds within 5 business days after receipt or mutual execution of lease, whichever later; all other (incl. earnest money) within 3 business days after receipt or mutual execution of contract, whichever later.
- 5.8 security deposit transfer: new firm discloses within 30 days; prior firm transfers within 60 days.
- 5.9 no diversion/conversion. 5.10 commingling prohibited; earned money withdrawn monthly; advances allowed if labeled; fees earned only when services complete; firm's own funds to cover bank charges allowed (5.14). No dollar cushion amount stated.
- 5.12 earnest money: broker delivers to earnest money holder named in contract, dated signed receipt; if firm holds, deposit per 5.7 (3 business days); may transfer to closing entity with dated signed receipt.
- 5.13 promissory note EM: tell seller due date; if unpaid, promptly notify seller and deliver original note.
- 5.14 journal (chronological) + ledger per beneficiary; no negative ledger; monthly three-way reconciliation (journal = ledgers total = reconciled bank balance); records at firm's place of business; may be electronic.
- 5.15 reports to beneficiaries per management agreement, or within 30 days after month end if silent.
- 5.19 branch trust account -> separate branch records. 5.21 produce records for audits; 5.22 employing broker jointly responsible.
- DRE page https://dre.colorado.gov/separate-accounts-and-accounting : fiduciary accounts protected from broker's creditors; Notice of Escrow or Trust Account form.

## Position 5 (interest) https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/Commission%20Position%205%20-%20%20Interest%20Bearing%20Trust%20or%20Escrow%20Accounts.pdf
- Interest belongs to owner of funds unless written agreement otherwise; contract/management agreement/lease should state who gets interest.

## Position 6 (release of EM) https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/Commission%20Position%2006%20-%20Release%20of%20Earnest%20Money%20Deposits.pdf
- Disburse per parties' agreement without unnecessary delay; signed release from both not required by Commission; not disciplined for holding disputed funds per contract; hold-harmless releases for broker prohibited; unclaimed -> State Treasurer under Unclaimed Property Act; title company/attorney holder -> broker not responsible for disposition.
- CBS1 sec 23 (from s3 notes): EM holder in dispute may hold, interplead, or after 120 days notice return to buyer if no suit filed.

## Listing/buyer contracts (2026 forms)
- ERTS and ERTB page 1: "COMPENSATION CHARGED BY BROKERAGE FIRMS IS NOT SET BY LAW AND IS FULLY NEGOTIABLE." If no relationship box checked -> transaction-broker.
- ERTB 7.3: seller/seller's firm may pay; buyer pays unpaid portion only if disclosed in writing before buyer signs contract; no added compensation from seller side without buyer's written consent.
- ERTS: listing firm MAY agree to pay buyer's firm (optional); holdover to Submitted Prospects named in writing during listing period.
- ERTS 5.5: no duty to independently inspect for buyer.
