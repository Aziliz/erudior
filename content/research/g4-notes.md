## CBSF1 — https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/Contract%20to%20Buy%20and%20Sell-Residential%20%28Foreclosure%29%20%28fillable_for%20use%20on%20or%20after%20January%201%2C%202026.pdf
- Form code CBSF1; adopted Aug 5, 2025; mandatory Jan 1, 2026. Conditions (residential, seller-occupied principal residence, buyer won't occupy, in foreclosure / 30+ days delinquent). Required: no assumption of seller obligations, no lease between parties, no repurchase option, Notice of Cancellation + Seller Warning attached, no consideration before cancellation right expires. Changing these voids contract. Not applicable if Short Sale Addendum part of contract.

## MHC1 — Manufactured Home Contract to Buy and Sell (Lot Lease Only) (2026 PDF on dre.colorado.gov)
- Code MHC1; adopted Aug 5, 2025; mandatory Jan 1, 2026. Manufactured home on a leased lot (park); seller is lot tenant; no land conveyed.
- Title by Certificate of Title (copy before UCC and Certificate of Title Deadline); buyer may run UCC search; after closing buyer submits title with Authentication of Paid Ad Valorem Taxes to Dept of Revenue.
- Contingent on Lot Owner written approval of buyer and home and lease assignment/new lease; buyer may terminate for unsatisfactory lease/approval terms; Lot Lease delivered by Lease Delivery Deadline; Lot Owner status letter requested ≥14 days before closing.
- Warns may not suit seller/broker required to register with Division of Housing (C.R.S. 24-32-3323).
- Seller financing: terms should be prepared by attorney or mortgage loan originator.

## MHA2 — Manufactured Home Addendum (2026)
- Code MHA2; adopted Oct 7, 2025; mandatory Jan 1, 2026. Attaches to the Land or Commercial contract when property includes a manufactured home; adds home description (serial no., VIN), certificate of title, UCC search, DOR title transfer.

## LB36-6-22 Licensee Buy-Out Addendum — https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/Licensee%20Buyout%20Addendum%20%28fillable%29.pdf
- Modifies Contract to Buy and Sell where the BUYER is a licensee; binds only signing licensee unless supervising broker of the brokerage working with Seller also signs.
- Deletes appraisal clause, liquidated damages, seller-default remedies. Seller may terminate any time by written notice (listing continues); seller reimburses buyer's out-of-pocket expenses up to stated amount; optional §8: contract is to help seller buy another property. Buyer represents sufficient resources; buyer bears resale profit/loss.
- History (vaned.com blog): removed Jan 1, 2019, reinstated Feb 2019.

## PCO70 Post-Closing Occupancy Agreement (2026) — dre.colorado.gov PDF
- Seller rent-back after closing; short-term residential occupancy, term not to exceed 60 days; longer -> residential lease. Rent paid to buyer in advance at closing; security deposit not over two months' rent, returned within 30 days after term per Colorado law. Utilities by checkbox. Buyer maintains major systems working at closing; seller keeps condition (normal wear excepted). Buyer access on 24 hours' notice. Seller may end early on 5 days' notice. Holdover -> eviction and damages. Buyer carries owner's insurance; seller may carry renter's. Consult legal/tax counsel.

## EX32-5-04 Exchange Addendum — dre.colorado.gov PDF
- Either buyer or seller may be the Exchanging Party (IRC §1031); other party cooperates at no additional cost or liability; Exchanging Party may assign contract rights; exchange is NOT a condition — contract binding whether or not exchange occurs; no dates changed. Get legal/tax advice.

## SWA35-8-10 Source of Water Addendum — dre.colorado.gov PDF
- Seller states: well (permit number, copy attached?), water provider (name/contact), or neither (describe source). Note some providers rely on nonrenewable groundwater; buyer may contact provider re long-term sufficiency.

## LC57 Exclusive Right-to-Lease Listing Contract (2026) — dre.colorado.gov PDF
- Landlord + Brokerage Firm; landlord agency or transaction-brokerage, default transaction-broker. Compensation on lease signed during listing period by anyone or ready/willing/able tenant; holdover to Submitted Prospect (Will/Will Not; unchecked = none); renewals/extensions by checkbox; sale commission only if box checked. Firm may accept security deposits and pass to Premises manager. Lead-based paint rental disclosure pre-1978; CO alarms; radon.

## CP-15 Leasing and Property Management — https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/Commission%20Position%2015%20-%20Leasing%20and%20Property%20Management.pdf
- No Commission-approved leases or management agreements; firm's own attorney drafts (Rule 7.1.B); using another firm's copy doesn't comply; owner-supplied lease = client form, keep written confirmation.
- ERTL: broker lists, advertises, screens, negotiates; role ends at lease signing. Exclusive Tenant Listing: tenant side, ends at lease execution.
- Property management = ongoing general agency; management agreement attorney-drafted; Brokerage Duties Addendum to Property Management Agreement used when broker provides leasing plus ongoing duties. Brokerage Disclosure to Tenant before services.

## BDA55-6-23 Brokerage Duties Addendum to Property Management Agreement
- Supplements a Property Management Agreement; landlord picks landlord agency or transaction-brokerage; uniform duties; lead paint, CO alarms, radon.

## AL12-6-22 Assignment of Lease
- Landlord selling rental property assigns lease to buyer at closing; security deposit credited/transferred; landlord notifies tenant; buyer assumes landlord obligations.

## SAA23 Seller Association Authorization (2026)
- Seller authorizes an Authorized Agent to request/receive association documents, status letters, construction defect documents; up to four associations.

## CBS1 §7 and §16.2 (2026 residential contract PDF on dre.colorado.gov)
- §7.1 Common interest community disclosure (bold): membership and bound by bylaws/rules; assessments; unpaid -> lien and possible sale; architectural approval; investigate finances, read governing docs.
- §7.2 seller provides Association Documents at seller's expense by Association Documents Deadline; may have association deliver.
- §7.3 docs: governing docs (declaration, articles, bylaws, rules, party wall agreements, governance policies); minutes; insurance list; assessments list (regular and special); financials (budget, statements, reserves, audit/review, closing fees list, reserve study); construction defect action notices from past 6 months.
- §7.4 buyer may terminate by Association Documents Termination Deadline in sole subjective discretion; late docs -> 10 days after receipt; never received -> by Closing; no notice = accepted/waived.
- §15.3 seller pays status letter fee; record change fees/working capital per checkboxes.
- §16.2: regular assessments prorated; prepaid credited to seller; reserves not credited to seller unless governing docs say; special assessments assessed before closing paid by Buyer/Seller checkbox; special assessment for improvements installed as of buyer's signature date paid by seller.

## C.R.S. 38-35.7-102 — https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_38-35.7-102
- Contracts for residential real property in a common interest community (on/after Jan 1, 2007) must contain a disclosure in bold-faced, clearly legible type, substantially in statutory form: membership/bound by declaration, bylaws, rules; financial obligations/assessments; unpaid -> lien, possibly sale; architectural approval; buyer should investigate finances and read governing documents.
- Seller's duty; failure -> buyer may sue seller for actual damages + court costs; affirmative defense: buyer had actual/constructive knowledge. Seller must on request provide or authorize association to provide governing/financial docs (association fee). Not for time shares.

## C.R.S. 38-33.3-316 lien — sources: communitypay.us summary; CAI https://www.caionline.org/advocacy/advocacy-priorities-overview/collecting-delinquent-assessments/priority-lien-colorado/ ; DRE HOA FAQ; CREM Ch.5 (legislative finding mentions six-month priority)
- Statutory lien for assessments and fines. Prior to all other liens except: liens recorded before declaration; first security interest recorded before assessment became delinquent; real estate taxes/governmental assessments.
- Super-priority: even over that first security interest for up to 6 months of common expense assessments based on periodic budget (would have come due, without acceleration, in 6 months before enforcement action).
- Foreclosure by association requires balance ≥ 6 months of common expense assessments and board recorded vote (CAI).
- Recording the declaration perfects the lien; 6 years to enforce (DRE FAQ).
- NOT used: 14-day statement of unpaid assessments (secondary source only, not confirmed on primary text).

## DRE HOA FAQ — https://dre.colorado.gov/hoa-frequently-asked-questions
- CAM licensing program July 1, 2015 – expired June 30, 2019 (HB19-1212 vetoed May 31, 2019). No current licensing of CAMs; no regulatory oversight of HOAs/CAMs.
- HOA Information & Resource Center (HB10-1278, effective Jan 1, 2011): information/education, registration, complaint data, annual report; NOT regulatory — no mediation, enforcement, fines, legal advice.
- Most HOAs register annually. Records under 38-33.3-317. Written fine policy with notice and hearing; collection policy (38-33.3-209.5 governance policies). Some CCIOA provisions apply only to communities created after July 1, 1992.

## CREM 2026 Ch.5 — https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/2026_CREM_Ch%2005.pdf
- HOA Center 12-10-801, continued through Sept 1, 2030 (SB25-184).
- Registration 38-33.3-401: annual; unregistered association cannot enforce assessment liens or use 38-33.3-123 remedies until registered; existing liens not extinguished, rights restored on registration.
- CAM licensing ended June 30, 2019.
- Definitions 38-33.3-103: declaration = recorded instrument creating CIC; bylaws; rules and regulations; common expenses.
- 38-33.3-104 CCIOA can't be varied/waived. 38-33.3-106.5 limits HOA restrictions (flags, signs, xeriscape, EV charging 106.8, etc.). 38-33.3-115 applies to CICs created on/after July 1, 1992.
