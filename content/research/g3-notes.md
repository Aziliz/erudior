# g3 research notes — Colorado settlement worksheet (verified 2026-10-12)

Builds on s4-notes.md (closing responsibility, good funds, doc fee statute, CBS1 title/proration defaults, taxes in arrears). Only new or re-checked facts here.

## Commission worksheet form — "Worksheet for Real Estate Settlement" (SS61-9-08, "WORKSHEET FOR CLOSING STATEMENT")
https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/Worksheet%20for%20Real%20Estate%20Settlement%20%28fillable%29.pdf
- Six columns: three pairs headed SELLER, BUYER, BROKER, each with Debit and Credit.
- Header: seller, buyer, property address, settlement date, date of proration, legal description.
- 54 lines incl. purchase price, earnest money deposit, new 1st/2nd loan, 1st/2nd loan payoff, taxes for preceding year(s), taxes for current year, lender fees/prepaids/reserves, owner's and lender's title premiums, OEC, certificate of taxes due, recording, documentary fee, transfer fee, HOA items, 2% Colorado withholding, FIRPTA, propane/fuel proration, rents, security deposits, seller concessions, broker's fee.
- Summary rows: Subtotals, Balance due to/from Seller, Balance due to/from Buyer, TOTALS. No written instructions; payer shown only by column used.

## Commission Closing Statement form (SS60-4-22) https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/Closing%20Statement%20%28fillable%29.pdf
- Per-party statement (seller's or buyer's; estimate or final) with DEBITS/CREDITS columns, same line items; signature lines for buyer/seller, brokerage firm and broker.

## DRE Closings (24 hours) qualifying education outline
https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/Closings%20%2824%20Hours%29%20Qualifying%20Education%20Outline.pdf
- Section II: "Introduction to a 6-column worksheet." III.C: calculating closing costs, prorations, prepaids, cash due to/from buyers and sellers. Section V: problems with assumptions (FHA, VA, conventional) and owner carry. Section I: good funds, withholding (nonresident, FIRPTA).

## Colorado Real Estate Manual (2009 ed., closings chapter; state publications library) — used for worksheet mechanics
https://spl.cde.state.co.us/artemis/regserials/reg52908r22internet/2009%20manual/reg52908r22200921internet.pdf
- Column pairs Seller / Buyer / Broker; broker column records money collected (debit) and paid out (credit).
- Earnest money: buyer credit, broker debit. New loan: buyer credit, broker debit. Payoff: seller debit, broker credit.
- Seller carry-back second: seller debit, buyer credit, no broker entry (seller receives it as future payments).
- Assumed loan: seller debit, buyer credit. Accrued interest on assumed loan (paid in arrears): seller debit, buyer credit.
- Adjustments between parties (tax proration, water) have no broker entry.
- Balance due to seller: seller debit (balancing) + broker credit. Balance due from buyer: buyer credit + broker debit. Each pair must then balance.
- Charges follow the contract and closing instructions; otherwise benefit/custom.
- Prorations: Commission contracts prorate to the closing date with the buyer owning the day of closing; manual uses 365-day/actual days unless stated; exams may use 360/30.
- Documentary fee: statute charges the person recording; manual's worksheet example debits the buyer. (Older manual; the broker-reconciliation rule it cites, "E-5(g)", is an old rule number — NOT cited in content.)
- RESPA/HUD-1 (pre-TRID): lender compliance doesn't satisfy Colorado broker closing statement duties. Content applies this to today's Closing Disclosure via current Commission rule 6.19 (s4 notes): broker must ensure accurate, complete closing statement signed by broker.

## Documentary fee — C.R.S. 39-13-102 (re-checked) https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_39-13-102
- $0.01 per $100 of consideration or major fraction; none at $500 or less; imposed on "every person offering for recording" a conveyance; collected by county clerk and recorder. Statute doesn't name grantor/grantee.
- Content wording: fee is imposed on whoever records the deed; deed is recorded for the buyer, so worksheet examples (Colorado manual) and exam problems charge the buyer; contract doesn't assign it, follow the problem.

## Title / taxes (secondary confirmation; primary in s4 notes)
- Owner's policy commonly seller-paid in Colorado (NFM Lending article, https://nfmlending.com/?p=26602) — consistent with CBS1 8.1.1 default.
- Land Title (LTGC) buyer statement explainer: buyer credited seller's portion of current-year taxes Jan 1 to closing because taxes paid in arrears. https://www.ltgc.com/articles/buyer-settlement-statements/
