# German B1: research notes (2026-10-17)

## Scope
- CEFR B1 global scale and self-assessment grid (coe.int Table 1; rm.coe.int/168045b15e).
- Goethe's official B1 topic and grammar lists are only in the paid Hueber handbook "Zertifikat B1 – Prüfungsziele, Testbeschreibung" (978-3-19-031868-1). Topics used here follow the ÖIF B1 matrix (https://www.integrationsfonds.at/fileadmin/user_upload/B1_OEIF-Einstufungsmatrix_modular.pdf) and the Goethe B1 Wortliste word groups.
- Core B1 grammar (ÖIF B1, Bard College GM201B, Portland CC GER 202, UNA GR 202, Uni Graz B1): productive Präteritum; Plusquamperfekt; nachdem/bevor/während/seit(dem)/bis; obwohl, damit, um/ohne/(an)statt … zu, da, falls; relative clauses in all cases with prepositions and wo/was (dessen/deren receptive); genitive and wegen/trotz/während/statt; passive present/past/with modals; full Konjunktiv II incl. past and unreal conditions; Futur I; n-declension; two-part connectors; verbs with prepositions and da-/wo- compounds.
- Borderline (taught, partly receptive): je … desto, adjectival nouns, participles as adjectives, sodass, brauchen nicht … zu.
- Deferred to B2 (ÖIF B2 matrix): Konjunktiv I / indirect speech (preview only), Zustandspassiv and passive alternatives, Futur II, indem, als ob (receptive only), nominalization, extended participial attributes.

## Vocabulary
Goethe B1 Wortliste (2016): "about 2,400 lexical units", including A1 and A2; receptive. https://www.goethe.de/pro/relaunch/prf/da/Goethe-Zertifikat_B1_Wortliste.pdf (copyrighted; used for size and word groups only).

## Exam (adult Goethe-Zertifikat B1, modular)
Lesen 65 min (5 parts, 30 items), Hören ~40 min (4 parts, 30 items), Schreiben 60 min (informal e-mail ~80 words, forum post ~80 words, semi-formal e-mail ~40 words), Sprechen ~15 min in pairs + 15 min preparation (planning together, presentation, reaction). Each module /100, pass 60; grades 90/80/70/60; modules can be taken and repeated separately; results within a year can be combined on request. Sources: durchfuehrungsbestimmungen_b1-1.pdf (from 1 Sep 2023), Prüfungsordnung (1 Sep 2025), goethe.de/ins/us/en/spr/prf/gzb1.cfm. Practice: b1_modellsatz_erwachsene.pdf, B1_Uebungssatz_Erwachsene.pdf, bfu.goethe.de/b1_mod.
