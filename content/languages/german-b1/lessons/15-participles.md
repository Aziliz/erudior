## What this lesson is for

This module has been about packing more description into one sentence: relative clauses (lessons 11 and 12) and the genitive (13 and 14). This last lesson adds two more tools, both of them about adjectives:

1. Verbs can become adjectives. *Das Kind weint* becomes *das weinende Kind* (the crying child); *Das Fahrrad wurde repariert* becomes *das reparierte Fahrrad* (the repaired bike). This is everywhere in written German, especially in adverts, instructions and news.
2. Adjectives without an article, now in all four cases, including the genitive (*wegen schlechten Wetters*), and after words like *viele, einige, mehrere, wenige*.

The theme is the language of products: adverts, online shops, delivery and customer reviews, which are full of exactly these forms: *kostenlose Lieferung*, *frisch gerösteter Kaffee*, *viele zufriedene Kunden*.

## Quick review: three sets of endings

You learned all the endings in German A2. The reference sheet for this module has the complete tables; here is the short version.

- After *der*-words (*der, dieser, jeder, alle*) the article shows the case, so the adjective takes a **weak ending**: only *-e* or *-en*: *der neue Laptop, mit dem neuen Laptop*.
- After *ein*-words (*ein, kein, mein …*) the adjective takes *-er/-es* where *ein* has no ending, otherwise the same as after *der*-words: *ein neuer Laptop, ein neues Handy*.
- With no article, the adjective has to show the case itself, so it takes a **strong ending**, the ending of the definite article: *neuer Laptop* (der), *frische Milch* (die), *kaltes Wasser* (das).

The one idea behind all three: somebody has to show the case and gender, either the article or the adjective. If the article does it, the adjective relaxes to *-e/-en*.

## Adjectives without an article, all four cases

| | masculine | feminine | neuter | plural |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| nominative | guter Kaffee | gute Qualität | gutes Material | gute Produkte |
| accusative | guten Kaffee | gute Qualität | gutes Material | gute Produkte |
| dative | gutem Kaffee | guter Qualität | gutem Material | guten Produkten |
| genitive | guten Kaffees | guter Qualität | guten Materials | guter Produkte |

Everything except the genitive is the article's ending (*der → -er, dem → -em, des → …*). The genitive masculine and neuter are the exception: not ~~-es~~ but *-en*, because the noun already has its *-s*: *wegen schlechten Wetters*, *trotz starken Regens*. Feminine and plural genitive are *-er*: *trotz guter Qualität*, *die Preise neuer Autos*.

In real life the genitive without an article is mostly written, and mostly after the prepositions from lesson 14: *wegen hoher Nachfrage* (because of high demand), *trotz intensiver Suche* (despite an intensive search), *innerhalb kurzer Zeit* (within a short time).

### After viele, einige, mehrere, wenige, ein paar and numbers

These words are not articles. They don't show the case clearly enough, so the adjective after them takes the strong endings, as if there were no article:

- *viele zufriedene Kunden* — many satisfied customers
- *einige neue Produkte* — some new products
- *mit mehreren kostenlosen Extras* — with several free extras (dative: *-en*)
- *nur wenige gute Bewertungen* — only a few good reviews
- *zwei kleine Pakete*; *ein paar praktische Tipps*

*viele, einige, mehrere, wenige* take the same strong ending as the adjective: *mit vielen neuen Funktionen*, *die Meinung einiger zufriedener Käufer*.

*mehrere* means "several" (more than two, not many); *wenige* means "few", the plural of *wenig*. *Wenige Kunden haben sich beschwert* — Few customers complained.

### After alle, sämtliche, beide: weak

*alle* behaves like *die*, so the adjective after it takes weak endings: *alle neuen Produkte*, *mit allen bestellten Artikeln*. *sämtliche* (all, every single one, formal) and usually *beide* work the same: *sämtliche reduzierten Waren*, *beide neuen Modelle*.

## Present participles: das weinende Kind

The **present participle** (Partizip I) is the infinitive + *-d*: *weinen → weinend*, *lachen → lachend*, *steigen → steigend*, *folgen → folgend*. It describes something happening at the same time, done by the noun itself, like English "-ing":

- *das weinende Kind* — the crying child (the child is crying)
- *steigende Preise* — rising prices (prices are rising)
- *kochendes Wasser* — boiling water
- *in der kommenden Woche* — in the coming week
- *die folgenden Fragen* — the following questions

Before a noun it takes normal adjective endings: *ein lachendes Mädchen, mit kochendem Wasser, wegen steigender Kosten*. On its own it works as an adverb: *Sie kam lachend ins Zimmer.* — She came into the room laughing.

Don't use it as a verb form: English "I am reading" is *Ich lese*, never ~~ich bin lesend~~. The German present participle is almost only an adjective or adverb. Some have become ordinary adjectives you already know: *spannend* (exciting), *dringend* (urgent), *passend* (suitable).

## Past participles as adjectives: das reparierte Fahrrad

The past participle you know from the perfect can also stand before a noun with adjective endings. It usually describes something that has been done to the noun (a passive, completed meaning):

- *das reparierte Fahrrad* — the repaired bike (someone repaired it)
- *frisch gerösteter Kaffee* — freshly roasted coffee
- *die bestellte Ware* — the ordered goods
- *ein gebrauchtes Auto* — a used car
- *das verlorene Portemonnaie* — the lost purse
- *Bitte geben Sie die ausgefüllten Formulare an der Kasse ab.* — Please hand in the completed forms at the till.

Compare the two participles of the same verb:

| Partizip I (doing, now) | Partizip II (done) |
|---|---|
| *die liefernde Firma* — the company doing the delivery | *die gelieferte Ware* — the delivered goods |
| *der lesende Mann* — the man who is reading | *das gelesene Buch* — the book that has been read |
| *ein schlafendes Kind* — a sleeping child | — |

A participle used like this is a **participial adjective**. With verbs that form the perfect with *sein*, the past participle has an active meaning: *der angekommene Zug* (the train that has arrived), *die gestiegenen Preise* (the prices that have risen).

### Longer phrases *(receptive)*

In written German, other words can come between the article and the participle:

- *die von uns hergestellten Produkte* — the products made by us
- *der seit Wochen angekündigte Streik* — the strike that had been announced for weeks
- *ein in Deutschland produziertes Gerät* — a device produced in Germany

English uses a relative clause or puts the description after the noun. To read these, find the article (*die*) and then jump to the noun (*Produkte*); everything in between describes it. You don't need to build long ones yourself; a relative clause does the same job: *die Produkte, die wir herstellen*.

## Products and shopping: the words

- *das Produkt, die Ware* (goods, merchandise; usually *die Waren*), *der Hersteller* (manufacturer), *herstellen* (to make, manufacture), *die Herstellung* (production).
- *hochwertig* (high-quality), *praktisch* (practical, handy), *umweltfreundlich* (environmentally friendly), *empfehlenswert* (recommendable).
- *das Sonderangebot* (special offer), *die Aktion* (promotion, special campaign), *begrenzt* (limited), *kostenlos, gratis* (free of charge), *der Kauf, der Käufer, die Käuferin*, *die Auswahl, auswählen* (choice, to choose).
- *die Bestellung, liefern, die Lieferung, die Lieferzeit, der Versand, die Versandkosten* (order, deliver, delivery, delivery time, shipping, shipping costs), *das Lager* (warehouse, stock), *vorrätig* (in stock).
- *die Rücksendung, zurückschicken* (return, to send back), *die Bewertung, bewerten* (review / rating, to rate).

Notes: *kostenlos* and *gratis* both mean "free of charge"; *frei* means free as in "not occupied" or "not restricted": *Der Platz ist frei. Der Eintritt ist frei.* *die Aktion* is a false friend: it's a promotion or campaign, not "action" in a film. *das Lager* is a warehouse or stock: *Der Artikel ist nicht auf Lager* — The item is out of stock.

## Text: Ein Produkt im Online-Shop

A product page for a coffee grinder, with two customer reviews.

> Kaffeemühle „Morgenrot“ – jetzt im Sonderangebot!
>
> Frisch gemahlener Kaffee schmeckt einfach besser. Unsere hochwertige Mühle aus rostfreiem Stahl wird in einer kleinen Werkstatt in Thüringen hergestellt. Sie ist leise, praktisch und dank ihres abnehmbaren Behälters leicht zu reinigen. Nur für kurze Zeit: kostenlose Lieferung innerhalb von drei Werktagen. Wegen großer Nachfrage ist die Stückzahl begrenzt.
>
> ★★★★★ Paula M.: „Ich hatte mehrere billige Mühlen ausprobiert, aber keine war so gut wie diese. Die bestellte Mühle kam schon nach zwei Tagen, gut verpackt und mit einer ausführlichen Anleitung. Absolut empfehlenswert!“
>
> ★★☆☆☆ Bernd K.: „Trotz guter Bewertungen bin ich enttäuscht. Die gelieferte Mühle hatte einen beschädigten Deckel. Die Rücksendung war zwar kostenlos, aber die neue Mühle ist leider erst in drei Wochen wieder vorrätig. Der Kundenservice hat nett geantwortet, aber für ein Produkt in dieser Preisklasse erwarte ich mehr.“

In English:

> Coffee grinder "Morgenrot" – now on special offer! Freshly ground coffee simply tastes better. Our high-quality grinder made of stainless steel is manufactured in a small workshop in Thuringia. It's quiet, practical and, thanks to its removable container, easy to clean. For a short time only: free delivery within three working days. Because of high demand, the number available is limited.
>
> Paula M.: "I'd tried several cheap grinders, but none was as good as this one. The grinder I ordered came after just two days, well packed and with detailed instructions. Absolutely recommended!"
>
> Bernd K.: "Despite good reviews I'm disappointed. The grinder that was delivered had a damaged lid. Sending it back was free, but unfortunately the new grinder won't be in stock again for three weeks. Customer service replied nicely, but for a product in this price range I expect more."

Notes: *mahlen* (to grind) has the participle *gemahlen*; *rostfrei* = stainless; *der Stahl* = steel; *dank* + genitive = thanks to; *abnehmbar* = removable; *der Behälter* = container; *die Nachfrage* = demand; *die Stückzahl* = number of items; *ausführlich* = detailed; *die Anleitung* = instructions; *beschädigt* = damaged; *der Deckel* = lid; *wird … hergestellt* is a passive (module 4). Find the past participles used as adjectives before a noun (there are four) and the adjectives without an article in the genitive.

## Pronunciation

- *hochwertig*: HOCH-wer-tig, stress on *hoch*; the final *-ig* sounds like *-ich*.
- *vorrätig*: FOR-rä-tig; *empfehlenswert*: em-PFEH-lens-wert.
- *gratis*: GRAH-tis; *Lieferung*: LIE-fe-rung.
- Present participles keep the stress of the verb: *WEI-nend, KO-chend, FOL-gend*; the *-d* sounds like *t* at the end, but like *d* before an ending: *weinende* (WEI-nen-de).

## Common mistakes

- Weak endings with no article: ~~mit viel frischen Obst~~ → *mit viel frischem Obst*; ~~gute Wein~~ → *guter Wein*.
- *-es* in the genitive: ~~wegen schlechtes Wetters~~ → *wegen schlechten Wetters*.
- Weak endings after *viele, einige*: ~~viele zufriedenen Kunden~~ → *viele zufriedene Kunden*. But after *alle*: *alle zufriedenen Kunden*.
- Forgetting the *-d*: ~~das weinene Kind~~ → *das weinende Kind*.
- Using the present participle for English "-ing" verbs: ~~Ich bin wartend.~~ → *Ich warte (gerade).*
- Mixing up the two participles: *die gelieferte Ware* (it was delivered), not ~~die liefernde Ware~~ (the goods are delivering something).

## Key points

- No article: the adjective takes the article's ending (*guter, gute, gutes, gutem*), except masculine and neuter genitive *-en*: *wegen schlechten Wetters*.
- After *viele, einige, mehrere, wenige, ein paar* and numbers: strong endings. After *alle, sämtliche, beide*: weak endings.
- Present participle = infinitive + *-d*, "-ing" meaning: *das weinende Kind, steigende Preise*.
- Past participle as adjective = done, finished: *das reparierte Fahrrad, frisch gemahlener Kaffee*.
- Both take normal adjective endings. Long participle phrases (*die von uns hergestellten Produkte*) are for reading.
