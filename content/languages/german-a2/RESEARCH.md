# German A2: research notes (2026-10-17)

## Scope
- CEFR A2 global scale and self-assessment grid (coe.int Tables 1–2; Europass grid PDF).
- Goethe's official A2 topic and grammar lists are only in the paid Hueber book "Goethe-Zertifikat A2 – Prüfungsziele, Testbeschreibung" (978-3-19-051868-5). The working topic list and grammar split in PROGRESS.md are derived from: the A2 model test, the Wortliste word groups, the ÖIF A2 matrix, and second-semester syllabi (Portland CC GER 102, MiraCosta German 102, U. Michigan German 102, Citrus College GER 102). US 102 courses overshoot A2 (genitive, passive, full Konjunktiv II are B1).
- Core A2: two-way prepositions and position verbs; dative verbs and object order; reflexive verbs; verbs with prepositions; Präteritum of modals (recognize others); weil/dass/wenn/ob, indirect questions; deshalb/trotzdem/sondern; adjective endings (nom/acc/dat); comparison; der-words and indefinite pronouns; Konjunktiv II as set phrases; possession with von and name-s.
- Borderline (taught as previews): relative clauses, zu + infinitive, als vs wenn, da-/wo-compounds, lassen.
- B1 (left out): Plusquamperfekt, productive Präteritum, genitive prepositions, Konjunktiv II past, passive, um…zu/damit, paired conjunctions.

## Vocabulary
Goethe A2 Wortliste (2016): "about 1,300 lexical units", including A1; recognition list; derivable compounds and derived nouns not listed. https://www.goethe.de/pro/relaunch/prf/de/Goethe-Zertifikat_A2_Wortliste.pdf (copyrighted; used for size only).

## Exam (adult Goethe-Zertifikat A2)
Lesen 30 min (4 parts, 20 items), Hören ~30 min (4 parts, 20 items), Schreiben 30 min (SMS 20–30 words; e-mail 30–40 words), Sprechen ~15 min in pairs. 100 points (written parts ×1.25 to 25 each). Pass: 60 overall, at least 45/75 written and 15/25 oral. Grades 90/80/70/60. Sources: A2_Modellsatz_Erwachsene.pdf, durchfuehrungsbestimmungen_a2.pdf (German version binding; the French says ×1.5).
