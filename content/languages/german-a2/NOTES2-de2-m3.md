# Round 2 notes: de2-m3 (Describing things)

## Honors lessons (honors: true, module de2-m3)
- `lessons/12h1-participles-place-names.md` — **de2-m3-h-participles**, "Beyond the three tables:
  participles, place names and adjectives as nouns" (~2,150 words, 45 min). Weak/mixed/strong
  declension names; past and present participles as adjectives (kochendes vs gekochtes Wasser, no
  "is -ing" in German, adverb before a participle has no ending); place-name adjectives in -er
  (capitalized, never inflected: mit Schweizer Käse); Berliner/Pfannkuchen/Krapfen and the Kennedy
  "doughnut" myth (labelled a myth); adjectival nouns Erwachsene/Jugendliche/Verwandte/Deutsche in
  all three tables vs ordinary nouns Österreicher/Schweizer. 3 worked examples, market dialogue,
  common mistakes, key points.
- `lessons/12h2-records-regions.md` — **de2-m3-h-records**, "Highest, biggest, longest: comparing
  the German-speaking countries" (~1,900 words, 45 min). Positive/comparative/superlative;
  intensifiers viel/etwas/noch (never sehr + comparative); um + exact difference; mit Abstand;
  doppelt/dreimal/halb so … wie, so viele vs so viel; zweitgrößte; einer/eine/eines der +
  superlative (genitive plural as a chunk); die meisten vs am meisten; mindestens/höchstens; so …
  wie möglich. Real geography (areas, populations, highest mountains, Rhine/Danube, Bodensee/Müritz,
  Bern as Bundesstadt), 2 worked examples, a reading text, a simplification note on "largest lake".
