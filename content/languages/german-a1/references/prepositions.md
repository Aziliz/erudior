## Articles after prepositions

| | masculine | feminine | neuter | plural |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| accusative | *den / einen* | *die / eine* | *das / ein* | *die / –* |
| dative | *dem / einem* | *der / einer* | *dem / einem* | *den / –* (noun + *-n*) |

Possessives and *kein* take the same endings: *für meinen Vater*, *mit meiner Mutter*, *ohne
mein Handy*, *mit meinen Kindern*, *keinem*, *keiner*.

## Prepositions with the accusative

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *durch* | through | *durch den Park* — through the park |
| *für* | for | *für meinen Bruder* — for my brother |
| *gegen* | against | *gegen die Wand* — against the wall |
| *ohne* | without | *ohne einen Koffer* — without a suitcase |
| *um* | around; at (clock time) | *um die Ecke* — round the corner; *um 8 Uhr* — at 8 |

## Prepositions with the dative

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *aus* | out of, from (origin) | *aus dem Haus* — out of the house; *aus der Schweiz* — from Switzerland |
| *bei* | at (someone's place, a company), near | *bei meinen Eltern* — at my parents' |
| *mit* | with, by (transport) | *mit dem Zug* — by train |
| *nach* | to (cities, countries); after | *nach Wien* — to Vienna; *nach dem Essen* — after the meal |
| *seit* | since, for (up to now) | *seit einem Jahr* — for a year |
| *von* | from, of | *von der Arbeit* — from work |
| *zu* | to (people, places) | *zum Arzt* — to the doctor |
| *gegenüber* | opposite | *gegenüber der Post* — opposite the post office |

*Seit* + present tense: *Ich wohne seit 2020 hier.* — I've lived here since 2020.

| Where to? (*Wohin?*) | Where? (*Wo?*) | Where from? (*Woher?*) |
|---|---|---|
| *zum Arzt* | *beim Arzt* | *vom Arzt* |
| *zu Anna* | *bei Anna* | *von Anna* |
| *nach Berlin* | *in Berlin* | *aus Berlin* |
| *nach Hause* | *zu Hause* | *von zu Hause* |

## Two-way prepositions: location with Wo? + dative

| Preposition | Meaning | Example (*Wo?* + dative) |
|---|---|---|
| *in* | in, inside | *im Bad, in der Küche* |
| *an* | on (a wall), at, by | *an der Wand, am Fenster* |
| *auf* | on (top of) | *auf dem Tisch* |
| *unter* | under | *unter dem Bett* |
| *über* | above, over | *über dem Sofa* |
| *neben* | next to | *neben der Tür* |
| *zwischen* | between | *zwischen dem Schrank und dem Regal* |
| *hinter* | behind | *hinter dem Haus* |
| *vor* | in front of | *vor dem Bahnhof* |

With movement to a place (*Wohin?*), the same prepositions take the accusative: *Ich lege das Buch
auf den Tisch.* — I put the book on the table. *Wir gehen ins Kino.* — We're going to the cinema.

## Contractions

| Long form | Short form | Case | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| *in dem* | *im* | dative | *im Zentrum* |
| *an dem* | *am* | dative | *am Montag, am Fenster* |
| *zu dem* | *zum* | dative | *zum Bahnhof* |
| *zu der* | *zur* | dative | *zur Post* |
| *von dem* | *vom* | dative | *vom Flughafen* |
| *bei dem* | *beim* | dative | *beim Arzt* |
| *in das* | *ins* | accusative | *ins Kino, ins Bett* |

## The imperative

| Infinitive | du | ihr | Sie |
|---|---|---|---|
| *gehen* | *Geh!* | *Geht!* | *Gehen Sie!* |
| *kommen* | *Komm!* | *Kommt!* | *Kommen Sie!* |
| *warten* | *Warte!* | *Wartet!* | *Warten Sie!* |
| *nehmen* (e → i) | *Nimm!* | *Nehmt!* | *Nehmen Sie!* |
| *sprechen* (e → i) | *Sprich!* | *Sprecht!* | *Sprechen Sie!* |
| *essen* (e → i) | *Iss!* | *Esst!* | *Essen Sie!* |
| *lesen* (e → ie) | *Lies!* | *Lest!* | *Lesen Sie!* |
| *fahren* (a → ä) | *Fahr!* | *Fahrt!* | *Fahren Sie!* |
| *schlafen* (a → ä) | *Schlaf!* | *Schlaft!* | *Schlafen Sie!* |
| *abbiegen* (separable) | *Bieg … ab!* | *Biegt … ab!* | *Biegen Sie … ab!* |
| *sein* | *Sei!* | *Seid!* | *Seien Sie!* |
| *haben* | *Hab!* | *Habt!* | *Haben Sie!* |

- du: the *du* form without *du* and without *-st* (or *-t* after *s, ß, z*). e → i/ie stays, a → ä
  goes. Stems in *-t/-d* keep *-e*.
- ihr: the *ihr* form without *ihr*.
- Sie: the *Sie* form with the verb first; *Sie* is never dropped.
- Separable prefixes go to the end: *Ruf mich an!* Add *bitte* to be polite: *Warten Sie bitte hier.*
