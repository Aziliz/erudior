- 25h1 says "many college texts and most analysis courses" define ln x as an integral, and
  attributes the nonelementary-antiderivative results to Liouville's work "in the 1830s and 1840s".
  It states lim Si(x) = π/2 without proof, labeled as a harder known fact.
- 25h1 says Si(π) "is the largest value of all"; true (the later maxima decrease toward π/2).
- 25h2 states the R_n − L_n identity only for monotone f and says so in Common mistakes.

  one example of sum-vs-integral trapping, before series.
