## For a reviewer
- 03h1 defines concave up by "secant segments on or above the graph" and states, without proof,
  that this matches "secant slopes increase". The inflection-point definition is the common one
  (continuous, concavity changes); the lesson notes textbooks vary.
- 03h1 Example 4 shows the cubic's point symmetry about its inflection point. The pc-m2 writer
  has an image named `pc-m2-cubic-center`, so their honors lesson may also cover the center of a
  cubic; mine approaches it through concavity. Worth checking the two don't clash.
- 03h1 says graphing calculators' numerical derivative "typically" uses the symmetric quotient
  (true of TI-83/84 nDeriv); hedged on purpose.
- The tax schedule in 03h2, its questions and the tiered-tax drill are made up and say so.
- q05 (range) states the solved-for-x formula in the stem so the blank asks only for the excluded y.
