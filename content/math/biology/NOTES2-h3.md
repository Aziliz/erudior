## For a reviewer to double-check

- **Illustrative numbers**: lesson 49's crab table (shaped on Elner and Hughes 1978, not their data), lesson 49's waggle calibration (1 s ≈ 750 m, stated as colony-specific), lesson 50's elephant-seal pup counts, lesson 51's picnic table and the town in the incidence example, and lesson 52's "5,000 species, 40 extinctions" calculation are invented teaching numbers and are labeled as hypothetical or illustrative in the text.
- **Clock-shift direction**: the lesson and question 10 use the simplified 15° per hour model. A 6 h fast shift gives about 90° counterclockwise and a 6 h slow shift about 90° clockwise (Northern Hemisphere); the lesson notes that real deflections are often smaller.
- **R0 values** (flu about 1.3, original SARS-CoV-2 about 2.5–3, measles 12–18) are given as approximate ranges. SIR final sizes (94% for $R_0 = 3$, 80% for $R_0 = 2$) come from the final-size equation, checked in Python.
- **Snow's grand experiment**: the per-10,000 rates are computed from Snow's house and death counts (315 and 37.5, ratio 8.4). Snow's own table prints 315 and 37. The rest-of-London row was left out because Snow's printed rate (59) doesn't match its counts (55.5).
- **Stewardship**: the lesson says to take antibiotics exactly as prescribed and notes that shorter courses work for many infections, rather than the older "always finish the course" message.
- **Contested points flagged in the text**: the haplodiploidy hypothesis and the inclusive-fitness debate, whether species-area extinction estimates are too high, and how strong the Yellowstone trophic cascade is.
- **Condor count** (about 600 by the end of 2025, more than half wild) comes from a single news source reporting 607 total and 392 wild. The panther numbers (20–30 before 1995, 8 Texas females, 120–230 now) come from University of Florida/IFAS.
- **Scratch files**: while previewing Mermaid diagrams I wrote d1–d4.mmd into the shared session scratchpad and then deleted them. If another writer had scratch files with those exact names there, those were overwritten. Nothing in the course folder was affected.
