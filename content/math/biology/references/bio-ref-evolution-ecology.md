## Population growth

| Model | Formula |
|---|---|
| Growth rate | $r = b - d$ (per-capita birth rate minus death rate) |
| Exponential | $\dfrac{dN}{dt} = rN$; in steps: $N_t = N_0 \lambda^t$ |
| Logistic | $\dfrac{dN}{dt} = rN\dfrac{K - N}{K}$; fastest growth at $N = K/2$ |
| Doubling time (approximate) | $t_d \approx \dfrac{70}{\text{percent growth per year}}$ |
| Mark and recapture (Lincoln–Petersen) | $N \approx \dfrac{M \times C}{R}$ (marked first time × caught second time ÷ marked recaptured) |

## Energy flow

About 10% of the energy at one trophic level ends up in the next (the range is roughly 5–20%). Energy at level $n$ ≈ producer energy × $0.1^{\,n-1}$.

## Diversity

Simpson's index of diversity: $D = 1 - \dfrac{\sum n(n-1)}{N(N-1)}$, where $n$ is the number of individuals of each species and $N$ the total; from 0 (one species) toward 1 (many species, evenly spread).

## Radioactive dating

Fraction left after $h$ half-lives: $\left(\tfrac12\right)^h$. Carbon-14 half-life ≈ 5,730 years; potassium-40 ≈ 1.25 billion years.

| Event | Time ago |
|---|---|
| Earth forms | 4.54 billion years |
| Earliest widely accepted fossils (prokaryotes) | about 3.5 billion years |
| Oxygen builds up in the atmosphere | about 2.4 billion years |
| First eukaryotes | about 2 billion years (estimates 1.6–2.1) |
| Cambrian explosion | about 539–515 million years |
| End-Permian extinction | about 252 million years |
| End-Cretaceous extinction (non-bird dinosaurs) | about 66 million years |
| *Homo sapiens* | about 300,000 years |
