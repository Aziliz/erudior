## Metric units

| Length | In meters | Typical size |
|---|---|---|
| millimeter (mm) | $10^{-3}$ m | a flea |
| micrometer (µm) | $10^{-6}$ m | bacterium 1–5 µm; animal cell 10–30 µm |
| nanometer (nm) | $10^{-9}$ m | membrane 7–10 nm; DNA width 2 nm |

1 mm = 1,000 µm; 1 µm = 1,000 nm. Volume: 1 mL = 1 cm³; 1 L = 1,000 mL. Mass: 1 g = 1,000 mg.

## Microscopes

| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| Total magnification | ocular × objective (e.g. $10\times \cdot 40\times = 400\times$) |
| Actual size | image size ÷ magnification |
| Field of view at a new power | old diameter × (old magnification ÷ new magnification) |
| Size from field of view | field diameter ÷ number of cells that fit across |

Light microscopes resolve about 0.2 µm; electron microscopes about 0.2 nm or better.

## Experimental design

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Independent variable | What the experimenter changes on purpose |
| Dependent variable | What is measured in response |
| Controlled variables | Everything kept the same between groups |
| Control group | The comparison group that doesn't get the treatment |
| Replication | Repeating trials or using many subjects so chance evens out |
| Hypothesis | A testable, falsifiable explanation or prediction |
| Theory | A broad explanation supported by many lines of evidence (cell theory, evolution) |
| Law | A description of what always happens, often as an equation |

## Working with data

| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| Mean | sum of values ÷ number of values |
| Percent change | $\dfrac{\text{new} - \text{old}}{\text{old}} \times 100\%$ |
| Rate | change in quantity ÷ change in time |

Graphs: independent variable on the x-axis, dependent on the y-axis; label axes with units; use a line graph for change over a continuous variable, a bar graph for categories.
