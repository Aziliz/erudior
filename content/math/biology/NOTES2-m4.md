## For a reviewer to double-check
- Knudson's mean of about 3 tumors per carrier (and so about 5% unaffected) is from his 1971 Poisson analysis, as summarized in teaching material; the noncarrier comparison in Example 2 is explicitly a simplified model, and the lesson says it underestimates sporadic cases.
- Trisomy 21 origin: about 90% maternal (about 70% of all cases maternal MI, about 20% maternal MII), about 5% paternal, a few percent postzygotic. Figures vary between studies.
- Maternal-age chart values (about 1/1,250 at 25, 1/950 at 30, 1/350 at 35, 1/100 at 40, 1/30 at 45) are commonly cited approximate live-birth figures, labeled approximate.
- "About 2% of people with Down syndrome are mosaic" (sources give 1–2%); XXY "about equally" paternal and maternal.
- "About 15% of flowering-plant speciation events involve ploidy change" (Wood et al. 2009).
- MPF amplification without protein synthesis (Wasserman and Masui 1975) is stated as "even with protein synthesis blocked".
- Immature frog oocytes are described as paused in prophase of meiosis I, "a G2-like state"; the injection picture labels them "paused before M phase".
- Triploid sterility uses an idealized 1:2 trivalent model; the lesson and drill say so.
- The m4-mitosis-stages anaphase drawing shows chromatids as straight rods, not V shapes (schematic).
