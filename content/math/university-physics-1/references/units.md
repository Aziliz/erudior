## SI prefixes

| Prefix | Symbol | Factor | Prefix | Symbol | Factor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| tera | T | $10^{12}$ | centi | c | $10^{-2}$ |
| giga | G | $10^{9}$ | milli | m | $10^{-3}$ |
| mega | M | $10^{6}$ | micro | μ | $10^{-6}$ |
| kilo | k | $10^{3}$ | nano | n | $10^{-9}$ |
| | | | pico | p | $10^{-12}$ |

## Derived SI units

| Quantity | Unit | In base units |
|---|---|---|
| Force | newton (N) | kg·m/s² |
| Energy, work | joule (J) | N·m = kg·m²/s² |
| Power | watt (W) | J/s |
| Pressure | pascal (Pa) | N/m² |
| Frequency | hertz (Hz) | s⁻¹ |

| From | To |
|---|---|
| 1 km/h | 0.2778 m/s (divide by 3.6) |
| 1 mi/h | 0.447 m/s |
| 1 cm² | $10^{-4}$ m² |
| 1 cm³ (1 mL) | $10^{-6}$ m³ |
| 1 L | $10^{-3}$ m³ |
| 1 atm | $1.013\times10^5$ Pa = 760 mm Hg = 14.7 lb/in² |

| 1 year | $3.16\times10^7$ s |

## Temperature

| Conversion | Formula |
|---|---|
| Celsius to kelvin | $T = T_C + 273.15$ |
| Celsius to Fahrenheit | $T_F = \tfrac95T_C + 32$ |
| Temperature differences | 1 K = 1°C = 1.8°F |

## Significant figures

| Operation | Keep |
|---|---|
| Multiply or divide | fewest significant figures among the inputs |
| Add or subtract | fewest decimal places among the inputs |
| Exact numbers (counts, defined conversions) | don't limit precision |
