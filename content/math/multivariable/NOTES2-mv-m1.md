## Added

**Honors lessons** (2, `honors: true`, `module: mv-m1`)
- `lessons/06h1-distances-identities.md`: mv-m1-h-distances, "Vector identities and distances in
  space" (45 min, about 1,900 words of prose). Covers the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality with the
  discriminant proof, the triangle inequality, cyclic symmetry of the scalar triple product, the
  vector triple product (BAC−CAB) with the geometric reason it lies in the plane of b and c,
  point-to-line distance and the foot of the perpendicular, skew-line distance as box height, and
  closest points from w·v₁ = w·v₂ = 0. Examples: checking BAC−CAB, Q(4,3,2) to a line (D = 3,
  foot (2,2,4)), skew lines with D = 3 and closest points (3,2,3), (5,1,1). Skew-lines SVG and a
  "choosing a distance formula" Mermaid chart.
- `lessons/06h2-quadratic-forms.md`: mv-m1-h-quadratic-forms, "Quadratic forms: tilted conics and
  quadric surfaces" (50 min, about 1,700 words of prose). Covers cross terms, the symmetric matrix
  of a form, rotation of axes with cot 2θ = (A − C)/B (derived), the invariants A + C and
  B² − 4AC, classification by the discriminant, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, the principal axes
  theorem (stated without proof and labeled so), the 2×2 eigenvalue formula, eigenvalue-sign
  classification of quadrics in space, and the link to the second-derivative test. Examples:
  5x² − 4xy + 8y² = 36 (eigenvalues 4, 9, semi-axes 3 and 2), z = xy as a 45° saddle,
  2xy + z² = 1 as a hyperboloid of one sheet. Plot of the tilted ellipse with principal axes and
  a Mermaid classification chart.
