## Added

**Honors lessons (2)**
- `lessons/04h1-symmetry.md` (a2-m1-h-symmetry, "Symmetry and the general transformation", 45 min,
  about 2,000 words of prose): even and odd functions (definitions, symmetric-domain requirement,
  odd ⇒ f(0) = 0, only the zero function is both), parity of products, the even/odd decomposition
  E = ½(f(x) + f(−x)), O = ½(f(x) − f(−x)) with proof of uniqueness, the general form
  a f(b(x − h)) + k with the point rule (x/b + h, ay + k), factoring f(bx − c), why the order of
  "scale" and "shift" changes the amounts, and symmetry of transformed graphs (line x = h for even
  parents, point (h, k) for odd parents). 4 worked examples, Common mistakes, Key points.
- `lessons/04h2-inverses-in-depth.md` (a2-m1-h-inverses, "Inverses of composite and piecewise
  functions", 45 min, about 1,800 words): (f∘g)⁻¹ = g⁻¹∘f⁻¹ with proof, monotonic ⇒ one-to-one (and
  1/x as a one-to-one, non-monotonic counterexample), how monotonicity combines, inverse values
  without a formula (x³ + 2x − 4), inverting piecewise functions (pieces one-to-one and ranges
  disjoint; ranges become the new conditions), a tiered electricity bill (made-up rates, labeled as
  such), self-inverse functions and the inverse of (ax + b)/(cx + d), and why an increasing function
  meets its inverse only on y = x (with −x³ as the decreasing counterexample). 5 worked examples.

  domain)". Stated that way on purpose.

  lesson.
