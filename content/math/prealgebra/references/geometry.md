## Between systems (approximate)

1 in = 2.54 cm (exact) · 1 m ≈ 3.28 ft · 1 mi ≈ 1.61 km · 1 kg ≈ 2.2 lb · 1 L ≈ 1.06 qt · 1 gal ≈ 3.79 L

## Perimeter and area

| Shape | Perimeter | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Rectangle | $P = 2l + 2w$ | $A = lw$ |
| Square | $P = 4s$ | $A = s^2$ |
| Parallelogram | sum of sides | $A = bh$ |
| Triangle | $P = a + b + c$ | $A = \tfrac12 bh$ |
| Trapezoid | sum of sides | $A = \tfrac12(b_1 + b_2)h$ |
| Circle | $C = \pi d = 2\pi r$ | $A = \pi r^2$ |

Heights are perpendicular to the base. $d = 2r$, $\pi \approx 3.14159$.

## Pythagorean theorem

In a right triangle with legs $a$, $b$ and hypotenuse $c$: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$.
Hypotenuse $c = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$; leg $a = \sqrt{c^2 - b^2}$.
Triples: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25 (and multiples).
