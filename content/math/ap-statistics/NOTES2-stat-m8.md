**Honors lessons** (3; the module's four core lessons leave three distinct directions, and the

- `lessons/36h1-power-and-sample-size.md` (stat-m8-h-power-planning, "Planning a study: power
  curves and sample size", 45 min, ~1,750 words): power curves (n = 25 vs. 100); derivation of
  n = ((z_α + z_β)σ/δ)², two-sided version; effect size d = δ/σ with Cohen's conventions labeled as
  rough; sample size for one proportion and for two means (per group); t tests need slightly more
  (noncentral t: 97 vs. 96); winner's curse / Type M error (Gelman and Carlin 2014); why observed
  power is pointless (Hoenig and Heisey 2001). Examples: n for power 0.90 at μ = 53 (96); the
  lesson-35 rider survey (power 0.72 at n = 300; 386 for power 0.80); lesson 36's two lines
  (44 per group); exaggeration ratio 2.49 at power 0.17.
- `lessons/36h2-equivalence-tests.md` (stat-m8-h-equivalence, "Showing that a difference is small:
  equivalence and non-inferiority", 45 min, ~1,600 words): equivalence margin, reversed hypotheses,
  TOST (Schuirmann 1987) with the intersection-union argument for using the full α, the 90%
  interval rule, the four interval outcomes (SVG), non-inferiority, bioequivalence (80–125% on the
  ratio, symmetric on the log scale), "which test" Mermaid. Examples: laser vs. manual rail gauge
  (n = 25, TOST p = 0.017, 90% CI (−0.017, 0.257)); the same data with n = 6 (inconclusive,
  ordinary p = 0.50); a non-inferiority trial for two proportions (z = 2.12, p = 0.017).
- `lessons/36h3-exact-tests.md` (stat-m8-h-exact-tests, "Exact tests: binomial, sign and
  signed-rank", 45 min, ~1,500 words): exact binomial test, the two two-sided conventions
  (doubling vs. R's binom.test), discreteness and conservative tests, the sign test, the
  Wilcoxon signed-rank test (exact enumeration), nonparametric tests, pointers to the permutation
  test (stat-h1 lesson 90) and Fisher's exact test (stat-m9 lesson 39h2). Examples: lesson 35's
  cancellations exactly (0.0819; two-sided 0.164 vs. 0.130); lesson 36's jig data by sign test
  (0.0625) and signed-rank test (W+ = 27, p = 2/128 = 0.0156), compared with the paired t (0.0074).
