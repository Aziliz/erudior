**Lessons** (module `stat-h1`; cross-cutting capstone topics chosen to avoid the core-module
honors lessons already written: 06h*, 10h*, 15h*, 18h*, 23h*, 27h*, 36h1, 39h*)
- `lessons/90-resampling.md` (stat-h1-resampling, "Resampling: the bootstrap and permutation
  tests", 45 min, ~1,600 words): bootstrap samples, bootstrap distribution, bootstrap SE, percentile
  interval; exact bootstrap SE of the mean $\sqrt{(n-1)/n}\,s/\sqrt{n}$; why percentile intervals are
  too narrow for small n (labeled simplified); exact permutation tests (extends lesson 20's
  randomization test); bootstrap vs permutation table; limits (biased samples, the maximum,
  dependence, tiny n). Examples: three hand resamples of 9 commute times; bootstrap SE (≈3.9) and
  95% percentile interval (15 to 33) of the median, with the long-run bootstrap distribution
  plotted; exact permutation test with C(8,4) = 70 splits, p = 2/70.
- `lessons/91-bayesian-inference.md` (stat-h1-bayesian, "Bayesian inference for a proportion",
  45 min, ~1,500 words): frequentist vs Bayesian; posterior ∝ prior × likelihood; three-value
  grid prior; beta prior, conjugate update Beta(a + x, b + n − x), posterior mean as weighted
  average (shrinkage); credible vs confidence intervals; rule of succession (Laplace 1774; Bayes's
  essay 1763 via Price); sequential updating; sensitivity analysis. Examples: grid posterior
  (0.023/0.298/0.679); flat prior → Beta(8, 4), mean 0.667, 95% CrI 0.390–0.891 vs Wald
  0.416–0.984; free-throw prior Beta(35, 15) + 9/20 → Beta(44, 26), mean 0.629, CrI 0.513–0.737.
- `lessons/92-multiple-testing.md` (stat-h1-multiple-testing, "Many tests at once: multiple
  comparisons and the replication crisis", 45 min, ~1,500 words): FWER, expected false positives,
  Bonferroni (proof via the union bound from 23h1), Holm mentioned, FDR and Benjamini–Hochberg,
  share of significant results that are false (Ioannidis 2005, labeled simplified), p-hacking
  (Simmons, Nelson & Simonsohn 2011; Gelman & Loken), the replication crisis (OSC 2015), ASA 2016
  statement, preregistration. Examples: 20 flavors (0.642); ten p-values: none/Bonferroni/BH =
  6/2/4 rejections; 1,000-hypothesis tree (36% false; 56% at power 0.35).
- `lessons/93-meta-analysis.md` (stat-h1-meta-analysis, "Effect sizes and meta-analysis:
  combining the evidence", 50 min, ~1,650 words): Cohen's d and pooled SD, why p-values aren't
  effect sizes ($t = d\sqrt{n/2}$), inverse-variance fixed-effect pooling, forest plots, Cochran's Q,
  I-squared, random-effects (DerSimonian–Laird, labeled "one common method"), publication bias,
  file drawer, funnel plots, history (Pearson 1904, Glass 1976, Cochrane 1993). Examples: d = 0.40
  with p = 0.066; four non-significant studies pooling to 2.50 (0.51 to 4.50), p = 0.014, I² = 0;
  three heterogeneous studies, Q = 7.95, I² = 0.75, τ² ≈ 6.3, random-effects 3.7 (0.4 to 7.0).
