## Which form?

Brokers must use the Commission-approved form whenever one exists for the situation. They may fill in blanks and add simple factual provisions, but may not draft legal language. Attorney-drafted documents are allowed when the parties' attorney prepares them.

| Situation | Form |
|---|---|
| Residential property | Contract to Buy and Sell Real Estate (Residential) |
| Residential income property | Contract to Buy and Sell Real Estate (Income-Residential) |
| Commercial | Contract to Buy and Sell Real Estate (Commercial) |
| Vacant land | Contract to Buy and Sell Real Estate (Land) |
| Residence in foreclosure (Foreclosure Protection Act) | The CFPA version of the residential contract. If there's a leaseback, repurchase option or loan assumption, an attorney must draft the contract. |
| Responding to an offer with changes | Counterproposal |
| Changing deadlines or terms after acceptance | Agreement to Amend/Extend Contract |
| Releasing earnest money | Earnest Money Release |
| Listing a property | Exclusive Right-to-Sell Listing Contract |
| Representing a buyer | Exclusive Right-to-Buy Contract |
| Selling a short sale | Short Sale Addendum |

## Disclosures that travel with the contract

- Seller's Property Disclosure (the seller's duty to disclose continues if new adverse facts come up)
- Lead-Based Paint Disclosure for housing built before 1978
- Square Footage Disclosure
- Source of Water Addendum
- Brokerage relationship disclosures

## How the residential contract works

- **Dates and deadlines** sit in one table at the front. Most contingencies are tied to a named deadline.
- **Time is of the essence.** Missing a deadline usually means losing the right tied to it.
- **Inspection:** the buyer either sends an Inspection Objection by the deadline or terminates by the termination deadline. If they don't act in time, they've accepted the condition.
- **Earnest money release:** when one party is entitled to the earnest money, the other party has 3 days after receiving the Earnest Money Release to sign it.
- **Notices** to a party's broker count as notice to the party, with exceptions; electronic delivery is allowed.
- **Default:** sellers can choose liquidated damages (keep the earnest money) or specific performance in the contract.
- **Disputes:** mediation is required before arbitration or a lawsuit, with exceptions in the contract.

> **Current forms:** The Commission revises its forms periodically. A set of revised forms becomes mandatory January 1, 2027. Always use the current version.
