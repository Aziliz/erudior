## Core formulas

| To find | Formula |
|---|---|
| Commission | Sale price × commission rate |
| Seller's net | Sale price − commission − loan payoff − seller's other costs |
| Price needed to net a target | (Desired net + seller's costs) ÷ (1 − commission rate) |
| Loan amount | Price (or appraised value, if lower) × LTV |
| LTV | Loan ÷ value |
| Points | Loan amount × number of points × 1% |
| Annual interest (simple) | Principal × annual rate |
| Monthly interest | Principal × annual rate ÷ 12 |
| Equity | Market value − debts against the property |
| NOI | Gross potential income − vacancy and collection loss − operating expenses (not debt service) |
| Cap rate | NOI ÷ value |
| Value by income approach | NOI ÷ cap rate |
| Gross rent multiplier value | Monthly gross rent × GRM |
| Percent profit | (Sale price − cost) ÷ cost |
| Property tax | Assessed value × mill levy ÷ 1,000 |

## The "IRV" triangle

Income = Rate × Value. Cover the one you want: Value = Income ÷ Rate, Rate = Income ÷ Value. The same pattern works for commission (Commission = Rate × Price) and interest (Interest = Rate × Principal).

## Land

- 1 acre = 43,560 square feet
- 1 section = 1 square mile = 640 acres
- 1 township = 36 sections = 6 miles × 6 miles
- Rectangular survey shortcut: multiply 640 by each fraction in the description. For example, the NE ¼ of the SW ¼ is 640 × ¼ × ¼ = 40 acres.
- 1 mile = 5,280 feet; 1 square yard = 9 square feet

## Prorations

1. Find the amount per day (or per month).
2. Count the days the party is responsible for.
3. Multiply. Decide who gets the credit.

| Item | Typical direction |
|---|---|
| Colorado property taxes (paid in arrears) | The seller hasn't paid this year's taxes yet: **debit seller, credit buyer** for the seller's days |
| Rent the seller already collected for the month | **Debit seller, credit buyer** for the buyer's days |
| HOA dues the seller prepaid | **Credit seller, debit buyer** for the buyer's days |
| Security deposits held by the seller | **Debit seller, credit buyer** (the buyer takes over the obligation) |

- The Colorado residential contract prorates to the closing date. Unless the parties choose otherwise, taxes are based on the calendar year before closing, using the most recent mill levy and valuation.
- The exam question will tell you the day count convention (365-day year or 30-day "banker's" months) and who owns the closing day. Read for it.

## Colorado-specific numbers

- **Documentary fee:** one cent per $100 of consideration, with a major fraction counted as $100. No fee if the consideration is $500 or less. A $485,000 sale: 4,850 × $0.01 = $48.50.
- **Nonresident withholding:** the closing company withholds the lesser of 2% of the sale price or the net proceeds when a nonresident sells. Nothing is withheld if the price is $100,000 or less, and there are exemptions by affirmation.
