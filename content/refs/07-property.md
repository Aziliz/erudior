## Forms of co-ownership

| | Tenancy in common | Joint tenancy |
|---|---|---|
| Shares | Can be unequal | Equal |
| On death | Share passes by will or inheritance | Passes to surviving joint tenants (right of survivorship) |
| Unities required | Possession only | Possession, interest, time, title |
| In Colorado | **Presumed** when a deed to two or more people says nothing | Must be expressly stated (for example, "as joint tenants" or "JTWROS") |

- Colorado doesn't recognize tenancy by the entirety. A deed that attempts one creates a joint tenancy instead.
- Colorado isn't a community property state.

## Estates

| Estate | Key point |
|---|---|
| Fee simple absolute | The most complete ownership |
| Fee simple defeasible | Can be lost if a condition is broken |
| Life estate | Lasts for a life; then goes to the remainderman or reverts |
| Estate for years | Lease with a definite end date |
| Periodic tenancy | Renews automatically (month to month) |
| Tenancy at will | No fixed term; ends by notice |
| Tenancy at sufferance | Tenant stays without permission after the lease ends |

## Deeds (most to least protection for the buyer)

| Deed | Warranties |
|---|---|
| General warranty | Covers title against defects from any time |
| Special warranty | Covers only defects arising while the grantor owned it |
| Bargain and sale | Implies the grantor has title, with no warranties against defects |
| Quitclaim | No warranties; conveys whatever interest the grantor has, if any |

## Recording

Colorado's recording statute is a **race-notice** statute: a later buyer who records first and didn't know about the earlier unrecorded deed wins.

## Land use

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Police power | Government's power to regulate for health, safety and welfare (zoning, codes) |
| Eminent domain | Taking private property for public use, with just compensation |
| Escheat | Property passes to the state when there's no will and no heirs |
| Variance | Permission to deviate from zoning |
| Nonconforming use | A use allowed to continue after zoning changes |
