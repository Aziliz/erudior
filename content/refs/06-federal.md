## Fair housing

| | Federal (Fair Housing Act) | Colorado adds |
|---|---|---|
| Protected classes | Race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, disability | Creed, sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression, marital status, ancestry, veteran or military status, source of income |
| File a complaint | HUD, within 1 year | Colorado Civil Rights Division, within 1 year |
| Lawsuit | Within 2 years | Within 2 years |

- **Federal exemptions:** a single-family home sold or rented by an owner without a broker or discriminatory ads; owner-occupied buildings with up to 4 units; religious organizations and private clubs (limited); qualifying housing for older persons (55+ or 62+). Hiring a broker ends the single-family exemption.
- The Civil Rights Act of 1866 bans all racial discrimination in property, with **no exemptions**.
- **Prohibited:** steering, blockbusting, redlining, discriminatory advertising, refusing reasonable accommodations (rules) or modifications (physical changes, usually at the tenant's cost).

## Lending laws

| Law | What to know |
|---|---|
| RESPA | No kickbacks or referral fees for settlement services. Commission splits between brokers are allowed. Affiliated business arrangements need disclosure. |
| TILA / Regulation Z | Trigger terms in ads (down payment amount, number of payments, payment amount, finance charge) require full disclosure: down payment, terms of repayment, and APR. |
| TRID | Loan Estimate by the 3rd business day after application. Closing Disclosure received at least 3 business days before closing. A new 3-day wait if the APR becomes inaccurate, the loan product changes, or a prepayment penalty is added. |
| ECOA | No credit discrimination by race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, age, receipt of public assistance, or exercising consumer credit rights |

## Disclosures and environment

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Lead-based paint | Housing built before 1978. Give the EPA pamphlet and known information, allow a 10-day inspection opportunity (can be changed or waived in writing), keep records 3 years. |
| Radon | EPA action level is 4 pCi/L |
| Flood | Special flood hazard areas (A and V zones) have a 1% annual chance of flooding; flood insurance is required with federally backed loans in those zones |

## Marketing and business practice

| Rule | What to know |
|---|---|
| Do-Not-Call | Check the registry every 31 days; call only 8 a.m. to 9 p.m.; established business relationship lasts 18 months after a transaction or 3 months after an inquiry |
| CAN-SPAM | Honor opt-outs within 10 business days; include a postal address |
| Antitrust | Never discuss commission rates with other firms (price fixing); no group boycotts, market allocation or tie-ins |
