# Language proficiency course tracks

Roadmap date: 2026-10-28. Courses are free to learn. These are **independent Erudior preparation courses**, not official exam-provider certifications or guaranteed equivalencies.

## Shared instructional contract

Each level is its own course with exam-level objectives, prerequisite and placement checks, progressive grammar and vocabulary, listening, reading, typed writing, pronunciation and recorded speaking practice, conversation dialogues, cultural context, adaptive quizzes, mock timed exams and a completion review. Separate **exam-readiness** from broader four-skill **communicative competence**. Retain sources and the revision date for every exam blueprint; do not claim exam affiliation. Validate language-specific fonts, bidirectional/transliteration where required, IME keyboard support and speech processing availability. Use authentic examples written or licensed for Erudior, not copied exam question banks.

## Special language decisions

* “Indian” names a country, not one language. Hindi is a placeholder pending selection; other languages (Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Urdu, Punjabi, etc.) may be separate tracks.
* Portuguese should offer Portugal-focused CAPLE and Brazil-focused usage from early levels; consider a separate Celpe-Bras readiness track if requested.
* Chinese should specify **Mandarin, simplified characters** as the default HSK-aligned track, with traditional-character literacy optional.
* Japanese is included because JLPT N5–N1 was explicitly cited as the requested competency-test model.
* Do not auto-label proficiency courses as school-grade credits or official professional certifications; distinguish independent preparation from authorized examination.
