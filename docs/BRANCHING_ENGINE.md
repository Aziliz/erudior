# Fictional branching scenarios and timelines

This is a reusable educational workspace for fictional cases and project milestones.
It supplies no legal, medical, financial or safety advice, real-world deadlines,
external filing, payments, credentials or automated professional decisions. Do not
enter real confidential client or patient information. No new course content is
included with the engine.

## Complete workflow

1. Author a scenario in a course or the supervising-adult workspace. Describe its
   fictional source, simulated time unit, state variables, decision nodes, conditions,
   effects, terminal outcomes and deadline milestones. Structured controls preserve
   the complete definition through save/reopen and portable course publication.
2. Import a course or portable definition into **Branching scenarios** in the unlocked
   adult area. Review the exact saved revision before assigning it. Imported approval
   labels cannot grant local authority. Course publication review and local scenario
   suitability review are distinct decisions.
3. Assign an exact reviewed snapshot to a current learner. Later author changes do
   not rewrite assigned cases, decisions or historical evidence.
4. Learners open their cases from Progress, inspect the current fictional case and
   state, make eligible decisions or explicitly advance simulated time, and explain
   their reasoning. The timeline records decisions, state consequences and milestones.
   Closing and reopening preserves the exact journal. Another profile cannot access it.
5. Submit an immutable checkpoint for human review, including incomplete or adverse
   outcomes. Further practice creates later evidence without rewriting earlier work.
   Human feedback remains separate from the simulator's computed outcome.
6. Export or print the historical evidence for local educational review. Check its
   source, privacy and review labels before sharing; local evidence is not an
   authenticated institutional submission or a credential.

## Determinism and limits

The engine accepts bounded declarative data, never JavaScript, VBA, external programs
or network actions. Typed numeric/Boolean state uses explicitly configured conditions
and effects. Graphs must be finite, reachable directed acyclic graphs with terminal
paths; loops are intentionally rejected rather than executed with hidden limits.

Time is an explicit bounded integer simulation clock with an author-chosen label.
It does not advance with browser elapsed time or a wall clock, and is unrelated to
instructional credit or real calendar rules. Choices consume their configured ticks;
learners may deliberately advance the simulation. A deadline is inclusive: arriving
at its tick is on time; a later tick misses it. Choice conditions use the state before
the action, then its tick cost and state effects apply. Overdue milestones are
processed by deadline, with authored order breaking ties, before arrival completion.
Each missed effect runs once, with every intermediate value checked against its
bounds. The timeline records the observation tick and identifies the due tick rather
than claiming an action happened earlier. Milestone deadlines and their
consequences belong to the exact assigned educational definition. A computed success
is not instructor approval, and a missed required milestone cannot become success
merely because the learner later reaches its node. The terminal narrative outcome
remains separately visible when required milestones make overall completion incomplete.

Existing historical date timelines, document cases, regulated transaction cases,
presentations, grades and mastery records are not changed by this engine.

## Authority, recovery and privacy

Adult writes require a freshly unlocked adult context bound to the current credential
and family membership. Learner writes recheck the selected profile. Serialized
compare-and-swap operations reject stale revisions; retries preserve command identity
rather than creating duplicate consequences or submissions.

Encrypted family backups retain exact source and evidence history. Restored approval
labels are historical and non-operative: all restored source packs require fresh local
review before further operative use. Fresh review provenance is recorded separately
without rewriting historical assignment snapshots. Full profile deletion removes its
cases, journals, submissions and feedback; reusable author definitions remain separate.

## Verification

Synthetic fixtures cover both a project workflow and a fictional transaction-style
workflow using the same engine. Release validation includes strict malformed-input and
replay tests, interrupted/repeated/concurrent actions, source revision isolation,
canonical/community save/publish/install parity, actual App authority, encrypted
recovery/deletion and real Chromium keyboard, narrow/wide, light/dark and accessibility
checks. No roadmap completion is claimed until exact release gates and live deployment
readback have passed.
