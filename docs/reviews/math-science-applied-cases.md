## Scoring and ambiguity checks

Each case uses three independently scored criteria worth 0, 1 or 2 points apiece. The rows above supply concrete evidence of setup, analysis and interpretation. For prealgebra, show the three comparisons 40 ≤ 44, 30 ≤ 32, 20 ≤ 21. For physics, take the cart–Earth system with gravitational potential zero at the bottom. For cooling, 30 minutes after the start is 20 minutes after the measurement. These explicit details earn all six points. A lone numerical answer can omit required modeling evidence even when correct. For example, a conditional-probability response that only gives 56.25% does not demonstrate the production table or explain the reversed condition. A seedling proposal that randomizes pots but keeps each whole light treatment on a single shelf still lacks independently replicated treatment units.

Four wording or scoring discrepancies were returned to the author and checked again. Geometry now defines the factor relative to the original length. The inverse-sensor criterion checks both forward stages. The trigonometry rubric also permits a fraction of a turn, which gives 75/360 × π × 12² = 30π and 48/360 × 2π × 15 = 4π. The cooling rubric now explicitly checks the initial condition and elapsed-time convention. All 48 criteria were inspected. This independent pass covered the sixteen new assignments, not the full existing banks, student interfaces, classroom deployment or student outcomes.
