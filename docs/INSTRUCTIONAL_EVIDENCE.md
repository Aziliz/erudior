# Instructional workload and assessment evidence

This is a **local educational evidence workflow**, not regulator-approved instructional
hours, verified enrollment, a grade, accreditation or a qualifying certificate. The
application supplies no current legal requirements or new course content. Providers
must obtain any necessary approval separately before offering qualifying credentials.

## Requirements → sources → observations → decisions

Open **Workload evidence** in the unlocked supervising-adult area. Author or import a
versioned requirements pack with jurisdiction/source/effective-date metadata, an
explicit equivalency method, requirement targets in integer minutes, optional passing
assessment thresholds, and mappings to exact installed lessons and assessment banks.
Each mapping has an allocated workload and a rationale. External activities require
an explicit author description rather than pretending to be installed course content.

Save, reopen and review the exact pack revision before assigning it to a current
learner. Editing creates a new revision; existing assignments and evidence keep their
original source and requirements snapshots. Imported approval labels are discarded.
Uninstalled, changed or ambiguously identified sources cannot silently become a new
operative assignment or capture. There is no artificial timer or waiting requirement.

Learners open **My instructional evidence** from Progress. Capture copies the current
mapped activity observation into immutable evidence, or records an explicitly labeled
outside-activity attestation. It does not alter lesson completion, study time, grades,
mastery, attendance, homeschool records or signed classroom submissions. Same-course, same-day recorded time is labeled ambient activity, not proof of the
mapped work. Unrelated reading and assessed-work records are not copied into exports;
no observation automatically awards equivalent workload. Manual homeschool time does not have a
reliable course identity and is not silently attributed to professional requirements.

Legacy lesson completion and assessment attempts do not retain historical lesson or
question source versions. Reports disclose this uncertainty: a current source snapshot
is not proof that the learner originally studied or answered that exact version.
Outside statements and local progress are not identity-verified submissions. A human
must inspect the raw evidence and its limitations, then explicitly approve, reject or
revoke a workload allocation with a rationale. Incomplete work and failed assessment
thresholds cannot receive approval. Threshold satisfaction is separate from workload
minutes and requires approved passing assessment evidence.

## Corrections, duplication and reports

Decisions append; they never overwrite earlier feedback or evidence. A later rejection
or revocation removes the earlier allocation from current totals while retaining the
audit history. A mapped source cannot earn duplicate workload for the same learner
through another evidence ID, assessment attempt, assignment or pack revision. Revoke
the previous allocation before deliberately reviewing it under a replacement mapping.
Approved minutes are bounded by the reviewed mapping; report requirement totals are
capped at their configured targets. Completing one requirement does not hide another
requirement's shortfall or missing assessment.

Portable JSON reports include exact assignment/pack/source snapshots, raw observations,
decision history, current educational totals and the limitations above. Review learner
names, notes and responses before sharing. Exports are not signed credentials or
authenticated remote hand-ins. Pack exports are reusable configuration without operative
approval. No regulator or institution receives a report automatically.

## Privacy and interrupted work

Adult access is bound to the explicitly unlocked credential and rechecked around
asynchronous operations. Learners can read and capture only their own assignments;
review authority is not exposed in their API. Compare-and-swap storage rejects stale
edits, and stable operation IDs prevent interrupted retries from duplicating evidence
or decisions. Full profile deletion removes its assignments, evidence and decisions
and leaves a deletion marker; reusable packs remain separate.

Encrypted family backups preserve history, but restored history is not authenticated.
Every restored pack and every restored decision is quarantined. A fresh pack review
does not reactivate imported educational credit: each evidence item needs a new explicit
human decision. Legacy backups without this feature clear its prior state rather than
leaving another family's records behind. Malformed or cross-family history is rejected
before restore writes begin.

Desktop installers, verified professional enrollment, official credential issuance,
regulator approval and real institution configuration are separate roadmap work.

Assessment mappings currently cover the entire selected bank. A subset practice or
sampled exam is retained as an incomplete observation and cannot satisfy the passing
gate. This prevents a short successful subset from being mistaken for the reviewed
full assessment. Initial and post-restore local pack reviews append reviewer/time
receipts separately from historical source approval labels.
