# Educational regulated-transaction simulations

This workspace is for **fictional educational practice only**. It does not establish
contract validity, supply legal or financial advice, hold money, execute payments,
file documents, award regulatory hours or issue credentials. Do not enter real
confidential transaction, client or financial information.

## Original capability and boundaries

The consolidated roadmap row combines contracts/counteroffers, client trust-account
reconciliation and complete transaction cases. It is not merely a branching story.
The reusable engine contains no shipped jurisdiction laws, official forms or course
content. A supervising adult provides and reviews the educational source material,
permission notes, jurisdiction/version/effective dates, document roles, contingencies
and structured rules. A pack's review is an educational decision, not regulator
approval or a statement that a rule is current law.

The generic branching/timeline engine, broader spreadsheet simulator, instructional
hour tracking and credential registry remain separate roadmap capabilities. Existing
signed classroom hand-ins and course-publication integrity are unchanged.

## Complete workflow

1. Create or import a reusable pack, select source document snapshots, configure
   the educational contract, deadline and trust-account rules, then save/reopen it.
2. Review and approve the exact saved pack revision. Imported pack or document
   approval labels never authorize use. Editing requires a new version and review.
3. Assign an exact pack snapshot to a current learner with a fictional start date.
   Updating a pack cannot silently change prior assignments or evidence.
4. Follow intake, representation, pricing, offers/counteroffers, explicit party
   decisions, title/inspection/financing outcomes, closing and archive. Record an
   unsuccessful outcome when a contingency fails or a case is terminated.
5. Post simulated client-owned deposits/disbursements and append-only reversals.
   Reconcile independent bank data, the cash register and reported client balances;
   inspect discrepancies rather than treating a label as regulatory compliance.
6. Save document answers and case actions, reopen interrupted practice, submit an
   immutable evidence snapshot and receive append-only instructor feedback.
   Revised submissions retain their original source/rule/event history.

## Monetary and date semantics

Money uses bounded integer minor units, not floating-point equality. Currency and
precision are explicit educational configuration. Client ownership is retained on
every entry; another client's balance never authorizes spending. Corrections append
references/reversals rather than editing posted history. Reconciliations identify
outstanding items explicitly and become stale after later ledger changes.

The case clock is an explicit simulated calendar date. Expiry, contingency and
funding deadlines are consequences of the selected educational pack, not legal
calendaring advice. Historical source versions remain visible even after expiry.

## Authority, privacy and recovery

The same-device adult and learner areas use the existing family/profile gates and
transactional named-state storage. An adult session is bound to the credential that
was explicitly unlocked. Fresh authority and learner membership are rechecked across
asynchronous writes. Compare-and-swap revisions reject stale windows; retries retain
identity rather than duplicating offers, funds or evidence.

Evidence exports are local educational records, not authenticated remote hand-ins.
Review identifiers, source permissions and privacy before sharing. Encrypted family
backups retain exact history, but restore is not proof of authenticity: every restored
pack requires fresh local review before further operative use. Profile deletion
removes its cases and evidence while reusable source packs remain separate.

## Where to open it

Use **Transaction practice** in the unlocked parent/teacher area. Learners open
**My educational transaction cases** on their progress page. Reusable source forms
come from the existing document/case studio; every pack reviews its copied sources
and complete rule configuration independently. No other store's imported approval
label is treated as transaction authority.

Closed or terminated case document responses are frozen. Settlement and corrected
ledger entries may still be recorded before archive, but changing the source forms
requires an explicit new assignment revision. An evidence checkpoint can be submitted
at an incomplete stage for instructor feedback; that does not imply successful
completion. Only the replayed outcome distinguishes an archived success from an
archived adverse outcome. Negative bank/claimed client balances remain visible audit
findings even when adjusted totals happen to match.
