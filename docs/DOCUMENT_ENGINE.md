# Interactive document and case file engine

This is a **same-device educational simulation workspace**, not an official form
service, legal or medical decision system, electronic-signature service, or filing
system. It does not submit anything to an institution or government, grant a
credential, or automatically change grades. Authors must use fictional or suitably
redacted educational material and respect their source's license.

## Complete workflow

1. An unlocked supervising adult creates a reusable template: source pages, source
   and licensing notes, stable fields with types/constraints and optional page-region
   overlays. Sources are plain text; no HTML, scripts, remote URLs or macros execute.
2. Save/reopen a draft, review its full contents, then explicitly approve that exact
   revision for educational use. Approval is not institutional/legal approval.
   Editing creates a new revision requiring a new approval. Imported templates lose
   approval and cannot carry another installation's authority into this one.
3. Assign a multi-document case to a current learner profile. Each case revision
   captures the exact approved source snapshots. Source updates do not mutate
   existing assignments or submissions; reassign a new case revision explicitly.
4. The learner opens only their own cases, fills typed fields and annotates exact
   source text ranges or page regions. Keyboard and text alternatives remain
   available alongside visual overlays. Save, reopen and resume a durable draft.
5. Submit a complete response. The original case instructions, source revisions,
   answers and annotations are immutable evidence. Revisions produce new evidence
   rather than rewriting previous submissions.
6. The adult reviews a selected immutable submission and appends feedback with an
   explicit reviewed/needs-revision decision. Feedback does not project into grades.
   Compare old and new source text, field values, annotations and feedback side by
   side to see what changed, including changes across explicit source reassignment.

## Persistence, recovery and privacy

The engine reuses the existing named-state transaction mechanism and native/web
compare-and-swap boundary. Saves use expected revisions; stale tabs do not silently
overwrite a newer draft or approval. Retry operations preserve identity, and live
adult authority/learner profile membership is rechecked across asynchronous work.
The learner projection excludes other learners and unassigned template drafts.

Encrypted family backup includes the bounded document library, exact source
snapshots and evidence. Restore validates references and learner membership before
writing. Profile deletion removes its cases, drafts, submissions and feedback and
retains a tombstone so stale writers cannot recreate the deleted learner's work.
Templates remain reusable educational resources; they are not learner progress.

Portable template files are source exchange only. Evidence exports are readable
local educational records, **not authenticated classroom hand-in files**. Files
leave the device only through an explicit export. Review answers, annotations,
feedback, source licensing and privacy before sharing; exported copies cannot be
remotely revoked. No new cloud storage, external credentials or third-party account
is configured by this capability.

## Scope boundaries

The original roadmap scope is fillable forms, document annotation, side-by-side
revision comparison, evidence submissions and instructor feedback, with approved
versioned templates and editable overlays. This standalone engine does not require
creating a course merely to work with a document. It serves both general education
and simulated professional learning through reusable, jurisdiction-neutral data.
No official legal/medical templates or new course content are distributed here.

Existing signed classroom hand-ins, immutable assessed-work V1 histories,
spreadsheet evidence, grading and canonical course publication remain unchanged.
This feature does not claim remote classroom transport, arbitrary PDF editing,
OCR, dynamic executable forms, signatures or an authenticated evidence exchange
protocol. Branching scenario decisions, deadlines and consequences remain the
separate roadmap capability. Desktop installer/version/tag work is deferred.

### Restored authority is not trusted
Restoring a backup preserves historical sources, evidence and feedback, but does not prove their authenticity. Every restored source is quarantined for fresh local adult review: historical approval text alone cannot authorize a new assignment or further learner writes. The studio labels restored history as local records, not authenticated events. Reapproval clears only that exact source revision's quarantine without rewriting the original evidence or historical approver/feedback text.
