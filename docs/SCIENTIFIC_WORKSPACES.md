# Scientific and engineering workspaces

## Scope and safety

This reusable educational workspace supports guided investigations, measurements,
uncertainty intervals, graphs, simulated instruments, engineering design iterations
and instructor-reviewed evidence. It is not laboratory equipment control, a safety
assessment, a calibration service, a professional engineering tool or a scientific
certification. Definitions require a supervising adult's local suitability review.
That review is not a guarantee that a physical experiment is safe. Do not enter
confidential information or follow a simulated result as real-world operational advice.
No new course content is included.

## Workflow

1. Author a versioned workspace in the course editor or adult studio. Describe the
   question, procedure, safety limits and model assumptions. Configure measurement
   channels with units and ranges, an uncertainty bound, graph axes, and an engineering
   design brief with explicit criteria. Save and reopen the exact definition.
2. Canonical and community courses preserve the same definitions through review,
   publishing and installation. Published identities and lesson references are stable;
   changes require a greater source revision. Portable sources cannot grant local
   approval. Review an installed or imported definition in the adult studio before
   assigning an exact snapshot to a current learner.
3. Record an investigation hypothesis, guided step notes, manual measurements or
   simulated trials, and a conclusion. Trials preserve their inputs and provenance.
   Corrections append a new trial rather than altering the old observation.
4. Explore engineering alternatives by capturing trials and documenting design
   iterations, rationale and tradeoffs. Compare the declared intervals against the
   source's criteria. A calculated pass/fail/uncertain result is not instructor
   approval or a certified design. Graphs have corresponding readable data tables.
5. Submit immutable evidence checkpoints, including incomplete investigations.
   Subsequent work does not overwrite the submitted source, journal or results.
   Human review and revision requests remain distinct from computed completeness.
   Export or print exact historical evidence only after considering its privacy.

## Numerical and scientific boundaries

The versioned instrument adapter uses bounded, finite parameters and existing pure
educational circuit and ideal-gas calculations. It does not execute learner programs,
imported formulas, VBA, device commands or network requests. Existing animated
simulations and isolated code exercises keep their original behavior; their transient
screen history is not silently converted into scientific evidence.

Circuit results assume ideal resistors and sources. Gas results use the existing
ideal-gas model and its stated constant and units; they do not establish how a real
gas or apparatus will behave. All calculations use JavaScript floating-point numbers,
not exact metrology. Model provenance and assumptions travel with the evidence.

A manually declared absolute uncertainty interval means the learner's stated range
around a measurement. It does **not** imply a normal distribution, confidence level,
calibration, an evaluated uncertainty budget or propagated statistical uncertainty.
Any simulated rounding allowance describes only that representation, not total
experimental uncertainty or real model accuracy. The tool makes no such inference.
NIST distinguishes uncertainty reporting and coverage interpretations from instrument
resolution; see [NIST reporting uncertainty](https://www.nist.gov/pml/nist-technical-note-1297/nist-tn-1297-7-reporting-uncertainty)
and [NIST resolution](https://itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/section4/mpc451.htm).
The ideal-model limitation is consistent with [NASA's ideal-gas description](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/equation-of-state-ideal-gas-2/).

## Evidence, authority and recovery

This is local educational evidence, not authenticated remote institutional hand-in.
Current unlocked adult authority and learner membership are checked again during
writes. Learner operations are bound to the current selected profile. Serialized
compare-and-swap updates, explicit expected revisions and stable retry identities
protect against concurrent changes and duplicate capture/submission.

Encrypted family recovery preserves exact source and historical evidence while
quarantining every restored source approval. Fresh local review appends a receipt;
it does not rewrite the historical assigned definition or impersonate an imported
reviewer. Malformed, foreign-family and unreplayable evidence is rejected before
restore writes. Full profile deletion removes private journals, submissions and
feedback, preserving reusable author definitions and deletion tombstones.

Existing guided labs and assessed-work V1 evidence, code work, simulations, branching
cases, spreadsheets and other historical artifacts are unchanged. No grades, mastery,
credentials or instructional credit are automatically granted. Desktop installers,
release version/tag changes and new course authoring remain deferred.
