Erudior uses an adaptation of KanjiVG release r20260714 (commit
d95a97627fd9fe5b2c8d06ca81e38149609c0c1e): the stroke paths of the 2,136 jōyō kanji and of
叱 填 剥 頬 were converted to Erudior's stroke format, scaled to a 0–100 square and rounded to whole
units, with stroke order and direction unchanged (src/lib/kanjiStrokes.data.ts, made by
scripts/build-kanji-strokes.mjs). That adapted data is licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0 as well. It is
downloaded only when a lesson traces kanji. The rest of Erudior is not covered by this license.
