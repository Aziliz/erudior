Notice of modification (section 2a): on 2026-11-17 Erudior made a modified copy of graphics.txt
from Make Me a Hanzi commit bddc96d41bef78427ed0e034e9f7e31d71fd1b92 (the first copy, for HSK
1-3, was made on 2026-11-16): it keeps only the 3,088 characters of HSK 1-9 (2025 exam syllabus)
and their Taiwan traditional forms from OpenCC (4,337 characters in all), split into one file per
HSK band (1-3, 4, 5, 6, and 7-9 in three parts); each stroke's outline path is kept with the
spaces after its command letters removed, and each stroke's median is converted from the 1024
box (y up) to a 0-100 square (y down) and rounded to 0.1. Stroke order (PRC order) and shapes are
otherwise unchanged. The modified files are src/lib/hanziStrokes.<band>.data.ts, made by
scripts/build-hanzi-strokes.mjs; both are freely available under the Arphic Public License in
Erudior's public source repository (https://github.com/Aziliz/erudior). A file is downloaded only
when a Chinese lesson traces one of its characters. The rest of Erudior is not covered by this
license.
