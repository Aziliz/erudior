Notice of modification (section 2a): on 2026-11-16 Erudior made a modified copy of graphics.txt
from Make Me a Hanzi commit bddc96d41bef78427ed0e034e9f7e31d71fd1b92: it keeps only the 655
characters of HSK 1-3 and their Taiwan traditional forms from OpenCC (923 characters in all);
each stroke's outline path is kept with the spaces after its command letters removed, and each
stroke's median is converted from the 1024 box (y up) to a 0-100 square (y down) and rounded to
0.1. Stroke order (PRC order) and shapes are otherwise unchanged. The modified file is
src/lib/hanziStrokes.data.ts, made by scripts/build-hanzi-strokes.mjs; both are freely available
under the Arphic Public License in Erudior's public source repository
(https://github.com/Aziliz/erudior). The data is downloaded only when a Chinese lesson traces a
character. The rest of Erudior is not covered by this license.
